# Review — Precision and Nuance
**C2-M1** — Revise quase-sinônimos e conotação, negação enfática e litotes, binômios e expressões fixas, e orações absolutas.

## O que você aprendeu neste módulo
- Escolher entre quase-sinônimos pela conotação e pela collocation
- Usar negação enfática e litotes (by no means, far from, not unlike)
- Usar e adaptar binômios e expressões fixas
- Condensar ideias com orações absolutas (The meeting over, ...)

## ✏️ Review exercises

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct option

_Choose the correct option._

1. (positive) Our grandmother was very ___ with money.  
   ( ) stingy · ( ) thrifty · ( ) tight-fisted
2. The outcome is ___ certain.  
   ( ) by no means · ( ) by any means · ( ) no means
3. They returned safe and ___.  
   ( ) sound · ( ) well · ( ) safe
4. ___, we'll eat outside.  
   ( ) Weather permitting · ( ) The weather permits · ( ) Permitted weather
5. She was ___ aware of the danger.  
   ( ) acutely · ( ) strongly · ( ) heavily

### Exercise 2 — Key word transformations

_Complete the second sentence using the word in CAPS (3–8 words)._

1. The holiday was not at all relaxing. (BUT) → The holiday was ___.
2. When the work was finished, we went home. (DONE) → ___, we went home.
3. Generally, the results were good. (LARGE) → ___, the results were good.

### Exercise 3 — Translate

_Translate into English._

1. A situação está longe de ser ideal.
2. Não foi pouca coisa.
3. Terminada a reunião, fomos embora.

## 🎤 Free conversation

Grave um áudio de 5 minutos no estilo "slow travel podcast": descreva uma viagem marcante com orações absolutas, use quase-sinônimos para pintar pessoas e lugares com precisão, comente com litotes e understatement e inclua pelo menos quatro binômios ou expressões fixas — sem soar forçado.

1. Which word in English do you find hardest to choose correctly?
2. Is understatement a form of politeness or of dishonesty?
3. Which English idiom do you use most often?
4. Describe a place you love in three sentences, using absolute constructions.
5. What does "precision" in language mean to you?
