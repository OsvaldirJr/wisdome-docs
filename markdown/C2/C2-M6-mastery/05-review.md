## ✏️ Review exercises

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct option

_Choose the correct option._

1. To check understanding politely:  
   ( ) Does that make sense so far? · ( ) Do you understand or not? · ( ) Got it, stupid?
2. To interrupt politely:  
   ( ) Sorry to cut in, but... · ( ) Shut up. · ( ) Wrong!
3. "Wherefore art thou Romeo?" means:  
   ( ) Why are you Romeo? · ( ) Where are you, Romeo? · ( ) Who are you, Romeo?
4. "I'm knackered" (UK) means:  
   ( ) I'm exhausted. · ( ) I'm angry. · ( ) I'm hungry.
5. In C2 key word transformations, you must use:  
   ( ) 3–8 words, with the given word unchanged · ( ) any number of words · ( ) the given word in any form

### Exercise 2 — Key word transformations

_Complete the second sentence using the word in CAPS (3–8 words)._

1. He wasn't at all pleased. (TOO) → He was ___.
2. In simple terms, the contract protects you. (PUT) → To ___, the contract protects you.
3. I've forgotten what I was saying. (TRAIN) → I've ___.

## 🎤 Free conversation

**Simulado de prova oral C2 (16 minutos, em dupla):** Parte 1 — conversa breve sobre você. Parte 2 — tarefa colaborativa com imagens ("What makes a society truly educated?"), discutindo e chegando a uma decisão. Parte 3 — long turn individual de 2 minutos sobre um cartão ("What role should tradition play in modern society?"), seguido de discussão com o parceiro e o examinador. Grave e avalie: precisão, nuance, flexibilidade, interação e prosódia.

1. What does it mean to truly master a language?
2. How has learning English changed the way you see your own language and culture?
3. Is mediation the most important language skill of the future?
4. What would you tell someone starting English from zero today?
5. What's next for you — with English and beyond?

## 🚀 Mini-projeto

**Projeto final do curso — "Full circle":** monte o portfólio completo da sua jornada: (1) o vídeo "This is me" do A1, (2) a palestra do B1+, (3) a apresentação do B2, (4) a palestra do C1 e (5) a **palestra final do C2 (8–10 minutos)**. Escreva um **ensaio reflexivo de 400 palavras** comparando os cinco momentos — fluência, precisão, registro, confiança e identidade — e termine com uma carta para um aluno que está começando o A1 hoje. Apresente numa cerimônia de encerramento. **Congratulations — and keep learning!**
