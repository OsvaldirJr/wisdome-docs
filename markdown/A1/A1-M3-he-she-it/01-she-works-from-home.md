## 🧩 Grammar: Simple present: he / she / it

Esta é a regra mais famosa do inglês básico: com **he, she, it** (ou um nome no singular), o verbo **ganha -s** na afirmativa.

- I work → **She works**
- They live → **He lives**

**Regras de ortografia:**
1. Regra geral: **+s** → works, plays, reads
2. Termina em **s, sh, ch, x, o**: **+es** → watches, teaches, goes, does
3. **Consoante + y**: troca por **-ies** → study → studies, cry → cries (mas play → plays, porque a antes do y é vogal)
4. **Irregular:** have → **has**

| I / you / we / they | he / she / it | Regra |
|---|---|---|
| work | works | +s |
| watch | watches | +es (ch) |
| go | goes | +es (o) |
| study | studies | consoante + y → ies |
| play | plays | vogal + y → +s |
| have | has | irregular |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L1/example-01.m4a) My sister lives in Lisbon. She works for a startup. — _Minha irmã mora em Lisboa. Ela trabalha numa startup._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L1/example-02.m4a) He studies at night and watches series on weekends. — _Ele estuda à noite e assiste séries nos fins de semana._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L1/example-03.m4a) The store opens at 10 and closes at 10. — _A loja abre às 10 e fecha às 22._

> 💡 **Dica:** Dica para memorizar: **"he, she, it — o S não pode faltar"**. É o erro mais comum, até em níveis avançados!

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ She work in a bank.  
  ✅ **She works in a bank.**  
  Com he/she/it, o verbo leva -s.
- ❌ He haves a car.  
  ✅ **He has a car.**  
  Have é irregular: has.
- ❌ My friends works here.  
  ✅ **My friends work here.**  
  O -s é só para a 3ª pessoa do singular. Friends é plural (they).

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Os três sons do -s final

O -s tem 3 pronúncias: **/s/** depois de sons surdos (works, speaks, posts) · **/z/** depois de sons sonoros e vogais (lives, plays, goes) · **/iz/** depois de s, sh, ch, x, z (watches, teaches, fixes) — esse ganha uma sílaba extra!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L1/pron-01.m4a) /s/ works · speaks
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L1/pron-02.m4a) /z/ lives · plays · knows
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L1/pron-03.m4a) /iz/ watches · teaches · uses

## 📖 Reading — A day in the life of a digital nomad [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L1/reading.m4a)

Thiago is 34 and he's a software developer. He doesn't have an office. He works from anywhere: today he lives in Bali, next month in Mexico City. He wakes up at 7, goes surfing and then works from a coworking space. He has meetings with his team in the evening because they're in Brazil. He loves his lifestyle, but he says it's sometimes lonely. He misses his family and his mom's food!

**Questions:**
1. What does Thiago do?
2. Where does he work?
3. Why does he have meetings in the evening?
4. What does he miss?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Gig economy

**Gig economy** é a economia dos trabalhos por demanda: motoristas de app, entregadores, freelancers. Uma pessoa que faz "bicos" ou trabalhos paralelos tem um **side hustle**: "She's a teacher, but she sells cakes as a side hustle."
