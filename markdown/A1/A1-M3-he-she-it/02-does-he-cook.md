## 🧩 Grammar: Does / doesn't

Na 3ª pessoa, o auxiliar **do** vira **does**. E aqui vem o segredo: **o -s passa para o auxiliar**, e o verbo principal **volta à forma base**.

- Afirmativa: She **works**. (o -s está no verbo)
- Negativa: She **doesn't work**. (o -s está no auxiliar)
- Pergunta: **Does** she **work**? (o -s está no auxiliar)

Nunca use o -s duas vezes: ❌ She doesn't works. ❌ Does she works?

**Wh- questions:** Where **does** he live? What **does** she do?

**Exceção:** quando **who** é o sujeito, não se usa does: **Who cooks** dinner? (Quem cozinha?)

| Afirmativa | Negativa | Pergunta | Resposta curta |
|---|---|---|---|
| He cooks. | He doesn't cook. | Does he cook? | Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. |
| She has a car. | She doesn't have a car. | Does she have a car? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |
| It works. | It doesn't work. | Does it work? | Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L2/example-01.m4a) My husband doesn't cook, but he does the dishes. — _Meu marido não cozinha, mas lava a louça._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L2/example-02.m4a) Does your roommate clean the kitchen? No, she doesn't! — _Sua colega de casa limpa a cozinha? Não!_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L2/example-03.m4a) Where does your brother work? — _Onde seu irmão trabalha?_

> 💡 **Dica:** **Does** também é o verbo "fazer" na 3ª pessoa: "He **does** the laundry". Em "What does he do?" o primeiro does é auxiliar e o do final é o verbo fazer.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ She doesn't works.  
  ✅ **She doesn't work.**  
  O -s já está no doesn't. O verbo volta à forma base.
- ❌ Does he has a car?  
  ✅ **Does he have a car?**  
  Com does, have volta a ser have.
- ❌ Who does cook dinner?  
  ✅ **Who cooks dinner?**  
  Quando who é o sujeito da pergunta, não se usa auxiliar.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Does e doesn't

**Does** é "dâz" (com som de z, não "dôus"). **Doesn't** é "dâzent" — e o T final quase some: "dâzen". Em perguntas rápidas, "Does he" vira "dâzi".

- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L2/pron-01.m4a) Does he = "dâzi"
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L2/pron-02.m4a) Does she = "dâzshi"
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L2/pron-03.m4a) She doesn't cook = "shí dâzen kúk"

## 📖 Reading — Who does the housework? [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M3-L2/reading.m4a)

Studies show that in many countries women still do most of the housework. In Brazil, women spend almost twice as many hours on chores as men, according to IBGE. But things are changing. Today many couples split the chores. In Ana and Rui's house, for example, Rui cooks and does the dishes, and Ana does the laundry and pays the bills. Their son, Theo, is 10. Does he help? Yes, he does! He makes his bed and walks the dog.

**Questions:**
1. Who does most of the housework in many countries?
2. What does Rui do?
3. Does Ana cook?
4. Does Theo help?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Chore chart

Em muitas famílias e casas compartilhadas nos EUA e no Reino Unido, é comum ter um **chore chart**: um quadro na geladeira com quem faz cada tarefa em cada dia. Crianças muitas vezes ganham uma **allowance** (mesada) por ajudar.

## ✅ Resumo
- Negativa: doesn't + verbo base.
- Pergunta: Does + sujeito + verbo base?
- O -s nunca aparece duas vezes.
- Who como sujeito: Who cooks? (sem does).
