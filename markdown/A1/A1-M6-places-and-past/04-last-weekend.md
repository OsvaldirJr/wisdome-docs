## 🧩 Grammar: Simple past: introdução

**1. Was / were** — o passado de to be:
- I / he / she / it → **was** · you / we / they → **were**
- Negativa: wasn't / weren't · Pergunta: **Was** it good? **Were** you at home?

**2. Verbos regulares** → **+ed**: watch → watch**ed**, play → play**ed**, visit → visit**ed**. Terminado em -e, só **+d**: like → like**d**, dance → dance**d**.

**3. Irregulares** — precisam ser memorizados. Comece pelos mais usados: go → **went**, have → **had**, do → **did**, see → **saw**, eat → **ate**, get → **got**, buy → **bought**.

**Boa notícia:** no passado, o verbo é **igual para todas as pessoas** (I went, she went, they went). Nada de -s!

**Perguntas e negativas** usam **did / didn't** + verbo base: **Did** you **go**? I **didn't go**. (No Nível 2 você vai aprofundar.)

| Presente | Passado | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| am / is | was | It was amazing! |
| are | were | We were at the beach. |
| watch | watched | I watched a movie. |
| go | went | She went to a party. |
| have | had | We had lunch with my parents. |
| see | saw | I saw my friends. |
| eat | ate | They ate pizza. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M6-L4/example-01.m4a) How was your weekend? It was relaxing. I stayed home and watched a series. — _Como foi seu fim de semana? Foi relaxante. Fiquei em casa e vi uma série._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M6-L4/example-02.m4a) Last Saturday we went to a concert. It was amazing! — _Sábado passado fomos a um show. Foi incrível!_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M6-L4/example-03.m4a) Did you go out last night? No, I didn't. I was tired. — _Você saiu ontem à noite? Não. Estava cansado._

> 💡 **Dica:** Expressões de passado vão geralmente no começo ou no fim da frase: **Yesterday**, I worked a lot. / I saw her **two days ago**.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ I goed to the beach.  
  ✅ **I went to the beach.**  
  Go é irregular: went.
- ❌ Did you went to the party?  
  ✅ **Did you go to the party?**  
  Depois de did, o verbo volta à forma base.
- ❌ In the last weekend...  
  ✅ **Last weekend...**  
  Com last, não se usa in the.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Os três sons do -ed

O -ed **quase nunca** é "éd"! Tem 3 sons: **/t/** depois de sons surdos (watched = "uótcht", worked = "uórkt") · **/d/** depois de sons sonoros (played = "plêid", stayed = "stêid") · **/id/** só depois de **t** ou **d** (visited = "vízitid", rested = "réstid") — só esse ganha sílaba extra.

- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M6-L4/pron-01.m4a) /t/ watched · worked · liked
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M6-L4/pron-02.m4a) /d/ played · stayed · loved
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M6-L4/pron-03.m4a) /id/ visited · rested · wanted

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "How was your weekend?" — a pergunta da segunda-feira

No trabalho, em países de língua inglesa, essa pergunta é quase obrigatória na segunda-feira. A resposta ideal é curta e positiva, com um detalhe: **"Good, thanks! I went hiking. How about yours?"** Sempre devolva a pergunta: **How about you? / And yours?**

- Regulares: +ed (3 sons: /t/, /d/, /id/).
- Irregulares: went, had, did, saw, ate.
- Did + verbo base para perguntas.
