## 🧩 Grammar: Wh- questions no simple present

Para fazer perguntas com **question words**, use a fórmula:

**Question word + do + sujeito + verbo + ...?**

- **Where** do you work?
- **What** do you study?
- **When** do you have English class?

Com o **verbo to be**, não use do: **Where** are you from? **What** is your name?

**Why x because:** a pergunta é com **why** (por que); a resposta é com **because** (porque). Em português é quase a mesma palavra, em inglês são duas diferentes.

| Question word | do | Sujeito | Verbo | Resposta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where | do | you | live? | I live in Recife. |
| What | do | you | do? | I'm a lawyer. |
| When | do | they | study? | At night. |
| Why | do | you | study English? | Because I need it for work. |
| Who | do | you | work with? | With my brother. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M2-L4/example-01.m4a) Where do you work? I work remotely for a company in Portugal. — _Onde você trabalha? Trabalho remotamente para uma empresa em Portugal._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M2-L4/example-02.m4a) What do you study? I study nursing. — _O que você estuda? Estudo enfermagem._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M2-L4/example-03.m4a) Why do you work at night? Because I study in the morning. — _Por que você trabalha à noite? Porque estudo de manhã._

> 💡 **Dica:** **What** + substantivo é "qual": **What** time? **What** course? **What** company?

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Where you work?  
  ✅ **Where do you work?**  
  Wh- questions no presente precisam de do.
- ❌ Where do you are from?  
  ✅ **Where are you from?**  
  Com o verbo to be não se usa do.
- ❌ Why you study English? — Why I want to travel.  
  ✅ **Why do you study English? — Because I want to travel.**  
  Why pergunta, because responde.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Entonação das Wh- questions

Diferente das perguntas de sim/não, as perguntas com **wh-** terminam com a voz **descendo**: "Where do you work? ↘". Já "Do you work? ↗" sobe. Isso deixa sua fala mais natural.

- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M2-L4/pron-01.m4a) Where do you live? ↘
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M2-L4/pron-02.m4a) Do you live here? ↗
- [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M2-L4/pron-03.m4a) What do you do? ↘

## 📖 Reading — Remote work: the new normal? [🔊](../../../audio/A1/A1-M2-L4/reading.m4a)

Remote work is very common today. Many people work from home two or three days a week — this is called hybrid work. Why do people like it? Because they save time and money on their commute. But there are problems, too: some people feel lonely, and some people work too many hours. Where do remote workers work? At home, in coffee shops, in coworking spaces — and some travel the world as "digital nomads".

**Questions:**
1. What is hybrid work?
2. Why do people like remote work?
3. What are two problems of remote work?
4. Where do remote workers work?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Small talk ou entrevista?

Em conversas casuais, perguntas como "Where do you work?" são normais. Mas evite perguntar idade, religião, salário ou estado civil para quem acabou de conhecer — em muitos países de língua inglesa isso soa invasivo, especialmente no trabalho.

## ✅ Resumo
- Wh- + do + sujeito + verbo?
- Com to be, sem do: Where are you from?
- Why pergunta, because responde.
- Wh- questions: voz desce no final ↘.
