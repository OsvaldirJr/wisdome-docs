## ✏️ Review exercises

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct option

_Choose the correct option._

1. You ___ washed the dishes — I was going to use the dishwasher. (você lavou)  
   ( ) needn't have · ( ) didn't need to · ( ) mustn't have
2. I hate ___ what to do.  
   ( ) telling · ( ) being told · ( ) to tell
3. She seems ___ the job. She looks very happy.  
   ( ) to get · ( ) to have got · ( ) having got
4. We need to ___ a decision today.  
   ( ) do · ( ) make · ( ) take part
5. The trip was ___ — everything was easy.  
   ( ) a piece of cake · ( ) an arm and a leg · ( ) a blessing in disguise

### Exercise 2 — Key word transformations

_Complete the second sentence using the word in CAPS (2–5 words)._

1. It was a mistake to leave so late. (SHOULD) → We ___ earlier.
2. It seems that he has lost his wallet. (SEEMS) → He ___ his wallet.
3. Perhaps she missed the train. (MIGHT) → She ___ the train.

## 🎤 Free conversation

**Simulado de prova oral (15 minutos, em dupla):** Parte 1 — responda 3 perguntas pessoais desenvolvendo cada uma. Parte 2 — fale 2 minutos sobre "a decision that changed your life". Parte 3 — discuta com seu colega "the most important skills for the future". Parte 4 — responda às perguntas abaixo. Grave e avalie: fluência, variedade, precisão e pronúncia.

1. What's something you needn't have worried about?
2. What do you hate being asked?
3. What's the most useful English expression you've learned?
4. What would you have done differently in your English learning?
5. What are your plans for your English after this course?
