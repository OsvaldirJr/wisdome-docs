## 🧩 Grammar: Essay structure and formal linkers

**Estrutura clássica (4 parágrafos):**

**1. Introdução** — apresente o tema com uma frase geral e mostre do que o texto vai tratar. Não dê sua opinião completa ainda (ou dê de forma breve, se a pergunta pedir).
"It is often said that university should be free for everyone. This essay will examine both sides of this question."

**2. Parágrafo 1** — **topic sentence** + argumento + exemplo/explicação.
"**Firstly**, free education would increase social mobility. **For instance**, ..."

**3. Parágrafo 2** — outro argumento **ou** o lado oposto.
"**On the other hand**, it could be argued that... **As a result**, ..."

**4. Conclusão** — resuma e dê sua opinião clara. Não traga ideias novas.
"**In conclusion**, **all things considered**, I believe that..."

**Linking words por função:**
- **Adicionar:** furthermore, moreover, in addition, what is more, not only... but also
- **Exemplificar:** for example, for instance, such as, a good example of this is
- **Causa:** because of, due to, owing to, since, as
- **Efeito:** as a result, consequently, therefore, thus, which means that
- **Contraste:** however, nevertheless, on the other hand, although, despite
- **Opinião:** in my view, from my point of view, I firmly believe that, it seems to me that
- **Concluir:** in conclusion, to sum up, all things considered, overall

**Linguagem impessoal** (soa acadêmica e objetiva):
- **It is often argued / claimed / believed that...**
- **There is no doubt that...** / **It is undeniable that...**
- Evite contrações (don't → do not), gírias e frases muito curtas em sequência.

**Dica de ouro:** **não exagere** nos conectores! Um por frase, no máximo — e use variedade.

| Função | Conectores |
|---|---|
| Ordem | Firstly, Secondly, Finally |
| Adição | Furthermore, Moreover, In addition, What is more |
| Exemplo | For instance, For example, such as |
| Causa | due to, owing to, because of, since |
| Efeito | As a result, Consequently, Therefore |
| Contraste | However, On the other hand, Nevertheless |
| Conclusão | In conclusion, To sum up, All things considered |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B2/B2-M5-L4/example-01.m4a) Furthermore, online learning allows students to study at their own pace. — _Além disso, o ensino online permite que os alunos estudem no próprio ritmo._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B2/B2-M5-L4/example-02.m4a) Owing to the high cost of living, many young people cannot afford to leave home. — _Devido ao alto custo de vida, muitos jovens não conseguem sair de casa._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B2/B2-M5-L4/example-03.m4a) It could be argued that exams do not measure real intelligence. Nevertheless, they remain the fairest way to compare candidates. — _Pode-se argumentar que provas não medem a inteligência real. Mesmo assim, continuam sendo a forma mais justa de comparar candidatos._

> 💡 **Dica:** A redação do ENEM e o essay em inglês são parecidos na estrutura (introdução, desenvolvimento, conclusão), mas o essay **não exige proposta de intervenção**. Já o **topic sentence** no início de cada parágrafo é muito mais valorizado em inglês.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Due to it was raining, the event was cancelled.  
  ✅ **Due to the rain, the event was cancelled. / Because it was raining...**  
  Due to + substantivo (não oração).
- ❌ In the other hand, ...  
  ✅ **On the other hand, ...**  
  A expressão é ON the other hand.
- ❌ Moreover, I think that is very bad. Moreover, it is expensive. Moreover...  
  ✅ **Moreover, ... In addition, ... What is more, ...**  
  Varie os conectores e não comece toda frase com um.

<details>
<summary>Transcript</summary>

**Examiner:** Many students think the most important thing in an essay is grammar. In fact, in the Cambridge B2 First, your writing is assessed on four criteria. First, content: have you answered the question and included all the required points? Second, communicative achievement: is the style appropriate — formal, in the case of an essay — and does your text hold the reader's attention? Third, organization: are your ideas logically connected, with clear paragraphs and a good variety of linking words? And finally, language: how wide and accurate is your vocabulary and grammar? So, as you can see, grammar is only one part. A text with a few small mistakes but excellent organization will often score better than a perfect text that doesn't answer the question. My advice? Spend five minutes planning before you write. It makes a huge difference.

</details>

## 📖 Reading — Model essay: Should university education be free? [🔊](../../../audio/B2/B2-M5-L4/reading.m4a)

In many countries, including Brazil, the cost of higher education is a controversial issue. While public universities offer free courses, places are limited and highly competitive. This essay will consider whether university education should be free for everyone.  
  
There are strong arguments in favour of free higher education. Firstly, it can increase social mobility. When students do not have to pay fees, talented young people from low-income families have a real chance of entering professions such as medicine or engineering. Furthermore, a more educated population tends to benefit the whole economy, as graduates generally earn more and contribute more in taxes.  
  
On the other hand, it could be argued that free education is not truly free, since it is paid for by all taxpayers, including those who never go to university. In addition, if universities receive too little funding, the quality of teaching may suffer. As a result, some experts suggest alternatives, such as income-based loans, where graduates repay their fees only when they earn above a certain salary.  
  
In conclusion, all things considered, I believe that higher education should remain free at public universities, as it is one of the most effective ways of reducing inequality. However, governments must ensure that it is adequately funded and that access is fair for students from all backgrounds.

**Questions:**
1. What does the introduction do?
2. What are the two arguments in favour?
3. What alternative do some experts suggest?
4. What is the writer's final opinion?

## 📝 Writing

Write a B2 First–style essay (140–190 words) answering this question: "Some people think that all students should learn to code at school. Do you agree?" Write about 1) future jobs, 2) creativity and problem-solving, and 3) your own idea. Use a clear 4-paragraph structure and formal linkers.

**Model:**

> Should all students learn to code at school?  
>   
> Technology plays a central role in almost every area of modern life. As a result, many people believe that coding should be a compulsory subject at school. This essay will consider whether this is a good idea.  
>   
> Firstly, digital skills are increasingly valued in the job market. Even professions that are not directly related to technology, such as marketing or medicine, now require people who understand how software works. Furthermore, learning to code develops problem-solving skills, as students must break complex problems into smaller steps and test different solutions.  
>   
> On the other hand, it could be argued that the school timetable is already full. If coding is added, other important subjects, such as art or physical education, may be reduced. In addition, many schools lack computers and trained teachers.  
>   
> In conclusion, I believe that all students should have the opportunity to learn basic coding. However, it should be introduced gradually and combined with other subjects rather than replacing them.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Redação do ENEM x Essay

Na **redação do ENEM**, o texto é dissertativo-argumentativo e termina com uma **proposta de intervenção**. No **essay** em inglês (Cambridge, IELTS, universidades), o foco é **argumentar com clareza e equilíbrio**, e o limite de palavras é rígido. Os textos em inglês também costumam ser mais **diretos**: frases mais curtas e uma ideia por parágrafo.

## ✅ Resumo
- 4 parágrafos: introdução · argumento 1 · argumento 2/contra · conclusão.
- Topic sentence no início de cada parágrafo.
- Varie conectores: furthermore, for instance, as a result, however, in conclusion.
- Formal: sem contrações, linguagem impessoal (It could be argued that...).
