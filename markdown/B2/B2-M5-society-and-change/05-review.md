## ✏️ Review exercises

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct option

_Choose the correct option._

1. The colleague with ___ I share an office is very quiet.  
   ( ) who · ( ) whom · ( ) that
2. The train was late, ___ meant I missed my flight.  
   ( ) what · ( ) which · ( ) that
3. ___ finished the course, she started looking for a job.  
   ( ) Having · ( ) Being · ( ) Had
4. ___ the cost, the project was approved.  
   ( ) Although · ( ) Despite · ( ) However
5. Owing ___ the strike, all classes were cancelled.  
   ( ) of · ( ) to · ( ) for

### Exercise 2 — Transform

_Rewrite the sentences as instructed._

1. I have three cousins. Two of them are doctors. (of whom)
2. After she had lived in Rio for years, she moved to Bahia. (Having...)
3. Although he was tired, he went to the meeting. (In spite of)

### Exercise 3 — Translate

_Translate into English._

1. O aluguel subiu muito, o que é um problema.
2. Algumas pessoas amam a cidade, enquanto outras odeiam.
3. Pode-se argumentar que provas são injustas.

## 🎤 Free conversation

Faça uma apresentação de 4 minutos sobre um problema social da sua cidade (moradia, transporte, desigualdade digital...): descreva a situação (relative clauses avançadas), conte como ela mudou (participle clauses), apresente os dois lados (contraste) e conclua com sua opinião (linkers formais).

1. What is the biggest problem in your city?
2. How has your neighbourhood changed in the last ten years?
3. Has technology made Brazil more equal?
4. What's the best way to reduce inequality in education?
5. Would you rather live in the centre or on the outskirts? Why?

## 🔑 Answer key

**Exercise 1:** 1. whom · 2. which · 3. Having · 4. Despite · 5. to

**Exercise 2:** 1. I have three cousins, two of whom are doctors. · 2. Having lived in Rio for years, she moved to Bahia. · 3. In spite of being tired, he went to the meeting.

**Exercise 3:** 1. The rent has risen sharply, which is a problem. · 2. Some people love the city, whereas others hate it. · 3. It could be argued that exams are unfair.

**Exercise 4:** 1. This is the city in which I grew up. · 2. Having said that, there are still problems. · 3. Technology is a double-edged sword.

**Exercise 5:** 1. Despite the rain, we went out. · 2. Walking home, I got caught in the rain. · 3. On the other hand, it is expensive.
