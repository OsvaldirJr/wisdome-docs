# Review — People and Perspectives
**B2-M1** — Revise estruturas de hábito, phrasal verbs de relacionamento, comparações avançadas e hedging.

## O que você aprendeu neste módulo
- Descrever hábitos presentes e passados com nuance (tend to, keep -ing, will/would)
- Falar de relacionamentos com phrasal verbs
- Fazer comparações sofisticadas
- Suavizar opiniões em temas sensíveis (hedging)

## ✏️ Review exercises

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct option

_Choose the correct option._

1. When we were kids, we ___ spend every summer at the beach.  
   ( ) would · ( ) used to be · ( ) tend to
2. He's ___ complaining about his job!  
   ( ) always · ( ) tends · ( ) would
3. Don't let ___.  
   ( ) down me · ( ) me down
4. It's ___ as easy as it looks.  
   ( ) nowhere near · ( ) far · ( ) much
5. The ___ you practice, the better you get.  
   ( ) more · ( ) most · ( ) much

### Exercise 2 — Mixed practice

_Complete with one word._

1. She comes ___ as shy, but she's very confident.
2. I really look up ___ my grandmother.
3. Houses are getting more ___ more expensive.
4. It could ___ argued that the policy has failed.

3. Entendo seu raciocínio, mas não estou totalmente convencido.

## 🎤 Free conversation

Grave um vídeo de 3 minutos sobre "As pessoas que me formaram": descreva duas pessoas importantes (personalidade e hábitos), como é sua relação com elas (phrasal verbs), compare sua geração com a delas e dê uma opinião sobre um tema delicado usando hedging.

1. How would your best friend describe you?
2. Who have you drifted apart from, and why?
3. How is your life different from your parents' lives at your age?
4. What stereotype about your generation do you disagree with?
5. How do you disagree with someone you respect?
