## 🧩 Grammar: Whose + possessive pronouns

Você já conhece os **possessive adjectives** (my, your, his, her...), que vêm **antes** de um substantivo: **my** phone.

Os **possessive pronouns** **substituem** o substantivo e ficam sozinhos: This phone is **mine**.

**Whose** pergunta "de quem": **Whose** bag is this? / **Whose** is this?

Também dá para responder com o **'s**: "Whose car is that?" — "It's **Paulo's**."

| Adjective (+ noun) | Pronoun (sozinho) | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| my | mine | It's my bag. → It's mine. |
| your | yours | Is this your key? → Is this yours? |
| his | his | It's his jacket. → It's his. |
| her | hers | It's her laptop. → It's hers. |
| our | ours | It's our car. → It's ours. |
| their | theirs | They're their dogs. → They're theirs. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L4/example-01.m4a) Whose suitcase is this? — It's mine. — _De quem é esta mala? — É minha._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L4/example-02.m4a) This isn't my charger. Mine is white. — _Este não é meu carregador. O meu é branco._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L4/example-03.m4a) Our apartment is small, but theirs is huge! — _Nosso apartamento é pequeno, mas o deles é enorme!_

> 💡 **Dica:** **Whose** e **who's** (who is) têm o mesmo som. Pense no contexto: "**Whose** is it?" (de quem) x "**Who's** there?" (quem está aí).

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ This is mine phone.  
  ✅ **This is my phone. / This phone is mine.**  
  Mine fica sozinho; antes de substantivo use my.
- ❌ Who is this bag?  
  ✅ **Whose bag is this?**  
  "De quem" = whose, não who.
- ❌ Can you borrow me your pen?  
  ✅ **Can you lend me your pen? / Can I borrow your pen?**  
  Lend = emprestar (dar); borrow = pegar emprestado (receber).

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Hers, yours, ours, theirs

O **s** final desses pronomes soa como **z**: hers ("rârz"), yours ("iórz"), ours ("áuerz"), theirs ("dérz"). E **ours** soa quase igual a **hours**!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L4/pron-01.m4a) It's hers, not yours.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L4/pron-02.m4a) Is it ours or theirs?
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L4/pron-03.m4a) Whose is it? It's mine.

## 📖 Reading — The sharing economy [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L4/reading.m4a)

Is it mine, yours or ours? Today, many people don't buy everything — they share. In some cities, bikes and scooters are everyone's: you use an app, ride, and leave them for the next person. Neighbors share tools in apps, and students borrow books from each other. Some people even lend their cars when they aren't using them. The idea is simple: why have it if you can borrow it?

**Questions:**
1. How do people use shared bikes and scooters?
2. What do neighbors share?
3. What is the main idea?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Borrow, lend e a cultura do "empresta?"

No Brasil, é comum pedir "empresta aí?". Em inglês, o pedido educado é **"Can I borrow...?"** ou **"Do you mind if I borrow...?"**. E quem pega emprestado costuma dizer **"I'll give it back tomorrow"** (devolvo amanhã).

## ✅ Resumo
- Whose...? = De quem...?
- my/your + substantivo · mine/yours sozinhos.
- mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.
- borrow (pegar) x lend (emprestar).
