## 🧩 Grammar: Question words + to be

Com o verbo to be, a pergunta é simples: **question word + to be + sujeito**. Sem do/does!

- **What** is your username?
- **Where** are my headphones?
- **Who** is that girl in your story?
- **Why** are you offline?

**How + adjetivo** cria perguntas muito úteis:
- **How old** is your phone? (Quantos anos tem...)
- **How far** is the airport? (Qual a distância...)
- **How long** is the video? (Qual a duração...)
- **How much** is the subscription? (Quanto custa...)

Respostas curtas com question words geralmente usam **it's** / **they're**: "Where are my keys?" — "They're on the table."

| Pergunta | Resposta |
|---|---|
| What's your username? | It's @mari_travels. |
| Where's the charger? | It's in my bag. |
| Who are they? | They're my coworkers. |
| How old is your laptop? | It's five years old. |
| How long is the video? | It's about ten minutes. |
| Why are you offline? | Because my battery is dead. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L2/example-01.m4a) Why are you on mute? We can't hear you! — _Por que você está no mudo? Não conseguimos te ouvir!_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L2/example-02.m4a) How much is the premium plan? It's $9.99 a month. — _Quanto é o plano premium? 9,99 por mês._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L2/example-03.m4a) Who's online now? Only Rafa. — _Quem está online agora? Só o Rafa._

> 💡 **Dica:** Em perguntas com **who/what** como sujeito, a resposta curta usa o verbo: "Who's ready?" — "**I am!**"

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Where is the keys?  
  ✅ **Where are the keys?**  
  Keys é plural: are.
- ❌ How many years is your phone?  
  ✅ **How old is your phone?**  
  Para idade (de pessoas e coisas), use how old.
- ❌ What is your senha?  
  ✅ **What's your password?**  
  Senha = password. Cuidado com o "portunglês"!

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Where's / What's / Who's

As formas contraídas são as mais naturais: **Where's** ("uérz"), **What's** ("uóts"), **Who's** ("rúz"). Atenção: **Who's** (who is) e **Whose** (de quem) têm o **mesmo som**!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L2/pron-01.m4a) Where's my phone?
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L2/pron-02.m4a) What's the password?
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M1-L2/pron-03.m4a) Who's online?
