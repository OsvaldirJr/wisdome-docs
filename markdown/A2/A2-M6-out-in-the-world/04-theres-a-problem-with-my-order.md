## 🧩 Grammar: A estrutura de uma reclamação educada

Reclamar bem em inglês é uma **habilidade social**. O segredo é ser **claro e educado** ao mesmo tempo — e usar tudo o que você aprendeu no A2:

**1. Chame a atenção com educação:** "Excuse me..." / "Sorry to bother you, but..."
**2. Suavize:** "**I'm afraid** there's a problem with..." / "**I think** there's a mistake..."
**3. Explique** (simple past + present continuous / present):
"I **ordered** the steak medium, but it**'s** very undercooked." / "The AC **isn't working** and the room **is too** hot."
**4. Peça uma solução** (could / would):
"**Could you** bring me another one?" / "**Would it be possible to** change rooms?"
**5. Feche bem:** "Thank you, I appreciate it." / "Thanks for sorting it out."

**Evite:** "This is horrible!" / "I want my money back now!" — soa agressivo e geralmente resolve menos.

| Passo | Frase modelo |
|---|---|
| 1. Atenção | Excuse me, sorry to bother you... |
| 2. Suavizar | I'm afraid there's a problem with my room. |
| 3. Explicar | I asked for a quiet room, but it's next to the elevator. |
| 4. Solução | Could I change to another room, please? |
| 5. Fechar | Thank you so much, I appreciate it. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M6-L4/example-01.m4a) Excuse me, I'm afraid my soup is cold. Could you heat it up, please? — _Com licença, infelizmente minha sopa está fria. Poderia esquentar, por favor?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M6-L4/example-02.m4a) I think there's a mistake on the bill. We didn't order any dessert. — _Acho que há um erro na conta. Não pedimos sobremesa._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M6-L4/example-03.m4a) The shower isn't working. Would it be possible to send someone? — _O chuveiro não está funcionando. Seria possível mandar alguém?_

> 💡 **Dica:** Respostas comuns do atendente: **"I'm so sorry about that."** / **"I'll fix that right away."** (will para decisão na hora!) / **"Let me talk to the manager."**

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ I want to make a reclamation.  
  ✅ **I'd like to make a complaint.**  
  Reclamação = complaint. Reclamation é recuperação (de terras, materiais).
- ❌ The food is cold! Change it!  
  ✅ **I'm afraid the food is cold. Could you change it, please?**  
  Imperativo direto soa rude com atendentes.
- ❌ The AC doesn't work now.  
  ✅ **The AC isn't working.**  
  Problema temporário/agora = present continuous.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Tom de voz calmo

Na reclamação, o **tom** importa tanto quanto as palavras. Fale devagar, com voz calma e entonação que sobe levemente nos pedidos: "Could you change it, please? ↗". Sorria — dá para "ouvir" o sorriso!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M6-L4/pron-01.m4a) Excuse me, I'm afraid there's a problem.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M6-L4/pron-02.m4a) Could you change it, please? ↗
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M6-L4/pron-03.m4a) Thank you, I really appreciate it.

## 📖 Reading — How to complain (and get results) [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M6-L4/reading.m4a)

A customer experience study found that most unhappy customers never complain — they just don't come back. That's bad for businesses, so many companies are happy when you complain politely! Experts give this advice: complain quickly, while the problem is happening. Talk to the right person — the manager, if necessary. Be specific: "The coffee was cold" is better than "Everything was terrible." Say what you want: a new dish, a discount, a refund. And stay calm: people help nice customers more.

**Questions:**
1. What do most unhappy customers do?
2. When should you complain?
3. Why should you be specific?
4. Why should you stay calm?

## 📝 Writing

Write a polite complaint review or email (80–100 words) about a real or invented bad experience: what happened, how you felt and what you want.

**Model:**

> Subject: Problem with my stay – Room 508  
>

> I stayed at your hotel from June 3 to 6. I'm afraid there were some problems. On the first night, the air conditioning wasn't working and the room was too hot. The next night, the room next door was very noisy. The staff were friendly and they changed my room, but I didn't sleep well for two nights. Would it be possible to get a small discount on my bill?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "Can I speak to the manager?"

Nos EUA, a frase **"Can I speak to the manager?"** virou meme associado a clientes arrogantes (o famoso estereótipo "Karen"). Use-a só quando necessário — e com educação. Na maioria das vezes, o próprio atendente consegue resolver se você for gentil.

## ✅ Resumo
- Excuse me → I'm afraid... → explicação → Could you...? → Thank you.
- complaint (não reclamation).
- Problema agora = present continuous (isn't working).
- Calma e educação resolvem mais.
