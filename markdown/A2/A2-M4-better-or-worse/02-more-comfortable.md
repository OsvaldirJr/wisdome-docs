## 🧩 Grammar: more / less + adjetivo longo · better / worse

**Adjetivos longos** (2+ sílabas, que não terminam em -y) usam **more** (mais) ou **less** (menos):
- "Lisbon is **more beautiful than** I imagined."
- "Remote work is **less stressful** for me."

**Irregulares** — os mais importantes de todos:
- good → **better** (melhor)
- bad → **worse** (pior)
- far → **farther / further** (mais longe)

**Igual e diferente:**
- **the same as**: "My phone is **the same as** yours."
- **different from**: "Life in Canada is **different from** life in Brazil."
- **similar to**: "Portuguese is **similar to** Spanish."

**Getting + comparativo** = ficando cada vez mais: "It's **getting better**." / "Prices are **getting more expensive**."

| Tipo | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| more + longo + than | Dubai is more expensive than Lisbon. |
| less + adjetivo + than | The bus is less comfortable than the train. |
| better than | This version is better than the old one. |
| worse than | The traffic today is worse than yesterday. |
| the same as | My answer is the same as yours. |
| different from | English is different from Portuguese. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L2/example-01.m4a) Living downtown is more convenient, but it's also more polluted. — _Morar no centro é mais prático, mas também é mais poluído._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L2/example-02.m4a) The cost of living in Portugal is better than in the UK. — _O custo de vida em Portugal é melhor que no Reino Unido._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L2/example-03.m4a) My English is getting better every day! — _Meu inglês está melhorando a cada dia!_

> 💡 **Dica:** Nunca use **more** e **-er** juntos: "more better" ❌ "more cheaper" ❌. É um ou outro.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ more better / more worse  
  ✅ **better / worse**  
  Better e worse já são comparativos.
- ❌ beautifuller  
  ✅ **more beautiful**  
  Adjetivos longos usam more.
- ❌ different of / the same that  
  ✅ **different from / the same as**  
  Preposições fixas: different from, the same as.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Better, worse e o 'T' americano

**Better** no inglês americano soa "bérer" (T como R rápido). **Worse** tem o som /ɜː/ de "bird" e "work": "uârs" — não "uórs". Cuidado: **worse** (pior) ≠ **words** (palavras).

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L2/pron-01.m4a) better · butter · water
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L2/pron-02.m4a) worse · work · word
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L2/pron-03.m4a) It's getting better, not worse.

## 📖 Reading — Small town or big city? [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L2/reading.m4a)

More and more Brazilians are moving from big cities to smaller towns. Why? Life in a small town is usually calmer, safer and less expensive. Rent can be three times cheaper, and the commute is shorter. With remote work, this change is more possible than ever. But small towns are different from big cities: there are fewer job opportunities, fewer hospitals, and life can be less exciting. For some people, it's the best decision of their lives. For others, it's lonelier than they expected.

**Questions:**
1. Why is life in a small town attractive?
2. Why is this change more possible now?
3. What are the disadvantages?

## 📝 Writing

Write a short comparison (80–100 words): "My city vs. my dream city".

**Model:**

> I live in Belo Horizonte, but my dream city is Barcelona. Barcelona is more beautiful and it has the beach — BH doesn't! The public transport is better and safer, too. However, Barcelona is more expensive and the rent is much higher. The food in BH is better, in my opinion: nothing is better than pão de queijo! And people in BH are warmer and more welcoming. Maybe the perfect city is BH with a beach. 😄

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Farther ou further?

Para distância física, os dois são aceitos (**farther** é mais americano). Para sentido abstrato (mais, adicional), use **further**: "**further** information" (mais informações), "**further** studies". Em e-mails: "For **further** questions, please contact..."

## ✅ Resumo
- more / less + adjetivo longo + than.
- good → better · bad → worse · far → farther.
- the same as / different from / similar to.
- getting better / worse = ficando melhor/pior.
