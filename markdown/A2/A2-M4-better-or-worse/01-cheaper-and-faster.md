## 🧩 Grammar: Comparativo: adjetivo + -er + than

Para comparar duas coisas com **adjetivos curtos** (1 sílaba ou 2 sílabas terminadas em -y):

**adjetivo + -er + than** → "The subway is fast**er than** the bus."

**Regras de ortografia:**
1. Regra geral: **+er** → cheap → cheap**er**, fast → fast**er**
2. Termina em **-e**: **+r** → nice → nice**r**, safe → safe**r**
3. Consoante-vogal-consoante: **dobra** → big → bi**gg**er, hot → ho**tt**er, thin → thi**nn**er
4. Termina em consoante + **y**: **-ier** → easy → eas**ier**, heavy → heav**ier**, happy → happ**ier**

**Graduar a diferença:** **much / a lot** (muito) e **a bit / a little** (um pouco) + comparativo:
"It's **much** cheaper." / "It's **a bit** slower."

| Adjetivo | Comparativo | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| cheap | cheaper | This plan is cheaper than that one. |
| nice | nicer | The new office is nicer. |
| big | bigger | São Paulo is bigger than Rio. |
| easy | easier | Android is easier for me. |
| heavy | heavier | My laptop is heavier than yours. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L1/example-01.m4a) The subway is much faster than the bus at rush hour. — _O metrô é muito mais rápido que o ônibus no horário de pico._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L1/example-02.m4a) This phone is a bit bigger, but the battery life is longer. — _Este celular é um pouco maior, mas a bateria dura mais._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L1/example-03.m4a) Is it cheaper to rent or to buy? — _É mais barato alugar ou comprar?_

> 💡 **Dica:** Depois de **than**, use **me, him, her, us, them** na fala informal: "She's taller **than me**." Na fala formal: "than I am".

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ more cheap / more big  
  ✅ **cheaper / bigger**  
  Adjetivos curtos usam -er, não more.
- ❌ It's faster that the bus.  
  ✅ **It's faster than the bus.**  
  Comparação usa than (e não that).
- ❌ bigest / easyer  
  ✅ **bigger / easier**  
  Atenção à ortografia: dobra a consoante (big) e y vira i (easy).

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Than fraco

**Than** é uma palavra fraca: soa como "dân" rápido, quase "dn". A força vai no adjetivo: "It's **CHEAP**er than the **BUS**." O -er também é fraco: "tchíp-âr".

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L1/pron-01.m4a) It's CHEAPer than the BUS.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L1/pron-02.m4a) She's TALLer than me.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L1/pron-03.m4a) It's MUCH FASTer.

## 📖 Reading — Train or plane? [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M4-L1/reading.m4a)

Is it better to take the train or the plane from London to Paris? The plane is faster in the air — only one hour and fifteen minutes. But the airports are far from the city centers, and you need to arrive early. The train takes about two hours and twenty minutes, from city center to city center. Train tickets are often cheaper if you buy early, and the seats are bigger. The train is also greener: its carbon emissions are much lower. For many travelers, the train is the smarter choice.

**Questions:**
1. Which is faster in the air?
2. What is a problem with airports?
3. Name two advantages of the train.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "Bigger is better"?
