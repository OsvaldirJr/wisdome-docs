## 🧩 Grammar: Past continuous + simple past

O **past continuous** descreve uma ação **em andamento** em um momento do passado. É o "estava fazendo".

**Estrutura:** was / were + verbo-ing
- I **was cooking** at 8 p.m. (às 20h eu estava cozinhando)

O uso mais comum é com o **simple past**, para mostrar uma ação longa (**background**) **interrompida** por uma ação curta:

- I **was cooking** (ação longa) **when** the phone **rang** (ação curta).
- **While** I **was walking** to work, I **saw** an accident.

**When** + simple past (ação curta) · **While** + past continuous (ação longa).

Duas ações longas ao mesmo tempo: "**While** I **was working**, my kids **were playing**."

| Ação longa (past continuous) | Ação curta (simple past) |
|---|---|
| I was driving | when my phone rang. |
| She was cooking | when she burned her finger. |
| While we were watching TV, | the power went out. |
| While he was running, | he fell. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L3/example-01.m4a) I was taking a shower when the doorbell rang. — _Eu estava tomando banho quando a campainha tocou._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L3/example-02.m4a) While she was texting, she walked into a pole! — _Enquanto mandava mensagem, ela bateu num poste!_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L3/example-03.m4a) What were you doing when the earthquake happened? — _O que você estava fazendo quando o terremoto aconteceu?_

> 💡 **Dica:** Stative verbs também não usam -ing no passado: "I **knew** the answer" (e não "I was knowing").

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ I cooked when the phone was ringing.  
  ✅ **I was cooking when the phone rang.**  
  A ação longa fica no past continuous; a curta, no simple past.
- ❌ While I walked to work, I was seeing an accident.  
  ✅ **While I was walking to work, I saw an accident.**  
  While + ação longa (past continuous); a interrupção no simple past.
- ❌ They was sleeping.  
  ✅ **They were sleeping.**  
  They usa were.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Was/were fracos no past continuous

Em "I was cooking", o **was** é rápido e fraco ("ái uâz kúkin"). A força vai no verbo principal: "I was **COOK**ing when the phone **RANG**."

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L3/pron-01.m4a) I was COOKing when the phone RANG.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L3/pron-02.m4a) We were WATCHing TV.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L3/pron-03.m4a) What were you DOing?

**Officer:** Can you tell me what happened?

**Witness:** Sure. I was waiting at the bus stop. It was raining a lot. A man was riding a scooter, and he was looking at his phone.

**Officer:** And the car?

**Witness:** The car was stopping at the traffic light. The man didn't see it, and he hit the back of the car. He fell, but he wasn't badly hurt.

**Officer:** What was the driver doing?

## 📖 Reading — Where were you when...? [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L3/reading.m4a)

Some moments are so important that people remember exactly what they were doing. On July 20, 1969, millions of people were watching TV when Neil Armstrong walked on the Moon. In 2014, many Brazilians were watching the World Cup semi-final when Germany scored seven goals. And in March 2020, a lot of people were working in their offices when they heard: "From tomorrow, everyone works from home." Psychologists call these "flashbulb memories".

**Questions:**
1. What were people doing when Armstrong walked on the Moon?
2. What happened in 2014?
3. What were many people doing in March 2020?
4. What are "flashbulb memories"?

## 📝 Writing

Write an "embarrassing moment" story (80–100 words) using past continuous and simple past.

**Model:**

> Last year, I was presenting a project in an online meeting with our clients from the US. Everything was going well. While I was talking, my son came into the room. He was wearing only his Spider-Man underwear and he was shouting, "Mom, I need help!" My camera was on, and everyone was watching. I turned red! But then the client laughed and said, "I have three kids. I understand!" We got the contract.

Pergunte a um americano **"Where were you on 9/11?"** e ele saberá responder com detalhes. No Brasil, eventos como o **7 a 1** ou o início da pandemia criaram o mesmo efeito. São ótimos temas para praticar o past continuous!

## ✅ Resumo
- was/were + -ing = estava fazendo.
- Ação longa (past continuous) + interrupção (simple past).
- when + ação curta · while + ação longa.
- Stative verbs: sem -ing.
