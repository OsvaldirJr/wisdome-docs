## 🧩 Grammar: Was / were + there was / there were

O passado do verbo to be tem só duas formas:
- **was** → I, he, she, it
- **were** → you, we, they

**Negativa:** wasn't / weren't · **Pergunta:** Was he...? / Were they...?

**There was / there were** = "tinha / havia" (no passado):
- There **was** a problem. (singular)
- There **were** many people. (plural)

**Expressões de passado:**
- **yesterday** (ontem), **yesterday morning**
- **last** + night/week/month/year/Friday (sem the!)
- tempo + **ago**: two hours ago, five years ago (ago vem **depois**)

| Afirmativa | Negativa | Pergunta | Resposta curta |
|---|---|---|---|
| I was tired. | I wasn't tired. | Was I late? | Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. |
| She was at work. | She wasn't at work. | Was she at work? | Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't. |
| They were happy. | They weren't happy. | Were they happy? | Yes, they were. / No, they weren't. |
| There was a party. | There wasn't a party. | Was there a party? | Yes, there was. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L1/example-01.m4a) Where were you yesterday? I was at the dentist. — _Onde você estava ontem? Estava no dentista._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L1/example-02.m4a) The concert was amazing, but there were too many people. — _O show foi incrível, mas tinha gente demais._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L1/example-03.m4a) I wasn't at the meeting. Was it important? — _Eu não estava na reunião. Foi importante?_

> 💡 **Dica:** Repare que **was/were** traduzem **era, estava, foi** ao mesmo tempo. "It was great" = Foi ótimo. "I was tired" = Eu estava cansado. "He was a teacher" = Ele era professor.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ They was at the beach.  
  ✅ **They were at the beach.**  
  They usa were.
- ❌ Had many people at the party.  
  ✅ **There were many people at the party.**  
  "Tinha" (existência) = there was/were, não had.
- ❌ I was there ago two years.  
  ✅ **I was there two years ago.**  
  Ago vem depois do período de tempo.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Was e were fracos

Na frase, **was** e **were** são fracos: "I wəz tired" ("ái uâz"), "We wər late" ("uí uâr"). Mas na resposta curta e na negativa são fortes: "Yes, I **WAS**." / "I **WASN'T**."

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L1/pron-01.m4a) I was at home.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L1/pron-02.m4a) We were late.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L1/pron-03.m4a) Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
