## 🧩 Grammar: Simple past completo

**Afirmativa:** verbo no passado — igual para todas as pessoas.
- Regulares: **-ed** (worked, played, studied, stopped)
- Irregulares: forma própria (went, got, bought) — veja a tabela de verbos no fim do módulo e **memorize em chunks** ("I got home", "I bought a...").

**Negativa:** **didn't** + verbo **base**
- I **didn't go**. She **didn't buy** it. (nunca "didn't went")

**Pergunta:** **Did** + sujeito + verbo **base**?
- **Did** you **see** the game? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
- **What did** you **do**? / **Where did** she **go**? / **When did** they **arrive**?

**Ortografia dos regulares:** study → stud**ied** (consoante + y) · stop → sto**pp**ed (CVC) · like → lik**ed** (termina em e: só +d).

| Afirmativa | Negativa | Pergunta |
|---|---|---|
| I watched a movie. | I didn't watch a movie. | Did you watch a movie? |
| She went to Rio. | She didn't go to Rio. | Did she go to Rio? |
| They bought a car. | They didn't buy a car. | What did they buy? |
| He got home late. | He didn't get home late. | When did he get home? |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L2/example-01.m4a) I left home at 7, but I forgot my laptop! — _Saí de casa às 7, mas esqueci o notebook!_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L2/example-02.m4a) Did you meet anyone interesting at the event? — _Você conheceu alguém interessante no evento?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L2/example-03.m4a) We didn't spend much money. We cooked at home. — _Não gastamos muito dinheiro. Cozinhamos em casa._

> 💡 **Dica:** Com **was/were** não se usa did: "**Were** you tired?" (e não "Did you be tired?"). Did é para todos os outros verbos.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ I didn't went to class.  
  ✅ **I didn't go to class.**  
  Depois de didn't, o verbo volta à forma base.
- ❌ Where you went?  
  ✅ **Where did you go?**  
  Perguntas no passado precisam de did (exceto com was/were).
- ❌ I meeted my wife in 2015.  
  ✅ **I met my wife in 2015.**  
  Meet é irregular: met.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Did you = "didja"

Na fala rápida, **did you** vira "**dídja**" e **what did you** vira "**uórdidja**" ou até "**uádja**". Reconhecer isso muda sua compreensão de séries e filmes!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L2/pron-01.m4a) Did you see it? ("dídja sí it")

**Questions:**

4. What happened to the wallet?

<details>
<summary>Transcript</summary>

**Officer:** OK, sir. When did you lose your wallet?

**Tourist:** This morning. I left the hotel at nine and I took the subway to the museum.

**Officer:** Did you buy anything?

**Tourist:** Yes, I bought a ticket at the museum. I paid with my card, and I put the wallet in my jacket.

**Officer:** And when did you notice?

**Tourist:** At lunch. I wanted to pay, but the wallet wasn't there.

**Officer:** Good news — someone found a wallet at the museum and brought it here. Is this yours?

## 📖 Reading — The day I met my best friend [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L2/reading.m4a)

I met my best friend, Lu, on my first day of college, fifteen years ago. I didn't know anybody, and I was very nervous. I sat in the last row. Then a girl came in late, sat next to me and asked, "Did I miss anything?" I said, "Only the teacher's name." We laughed, and after class we went for coffee. We talked for three hours! We didn't know it then, but that day changed our lives. Last year, I was her bridesmaid.

**Questions:**
1. When did the writer meet Lu?
2. Where did the writer sit?
3. What did they do after class?
4. What happened last year?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Storytelling no trabalho

Em entrevistas de emprego em inglês, é comum ouvir **"Tell me about a time when you..."** (fale de uma vez em que você...). A resposta esperada é uma história no **simple past** seguindo o método **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

## ✅ Resumo
- Afirmativa: -ed ou irregular.
- Negativa: didn't + base.
- Pergunta: (Wh-) + did + sujeito + base?
- Com was/were, sem did.
