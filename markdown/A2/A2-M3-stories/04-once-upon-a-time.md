## 🧩 Grammar: Conectores de narrativa

Uma boa história em inglês tem **estrutura** e **conectores**:

**1. Começo (cenário)** — past continuous + was/were:
"It **was** a rainy Friday. I **was driving** home..."
**2. Problema** — simple past + **suddenly**:
"**Suddenly**, the car **stopped**."
**3. Sequência** — **then, after that, so, because, but**:
"I **called** my brother, **but** he didn't answer, **so** I **walked**."
**4. Final** — **luckily / unfortunately, in the end, finally**:
"**In the end**, a nice guy **helped** me."

**So x because:** so mostra a **consequência** (então); because mostra a **causa** (porque).
- It was late, **so** I took a taxi. / I took a taxi **because** it was late.

**Reagir** também faz parte: Really? / No way! / Oh no! / Then what? / What happened next?

| Função | Conectores |
|---|---|
| Começo | One day... / It all started when... / At first... |
| Sequência | Then... / After that... / Later... |
| Surpresa | Suddenly... / All of a sudden... |
| Causa / consequência | because... / so... |
| Contraste | but... / However,... |
| Final | Luckily... / Unfortunately... / In the end... / Finally... |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L4/example-01.m4a) It all started when I was waiting for my flight in Lima. — _Tudo começou quando eu esperava meu voo em Lima._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L4/example-02.m4a) The flight was cancelled, so I stayed one more night. — _O voo foi cancelado, então fiquei mais uma noite._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L4/example-03.m4a) Unfortunately, my bag didn't arrive. Luckily, I had my passport. — _Infelizmente, minha mala não chegou. Por sorte, eu estava com meu passaporte._

> 💡 **Dica:** **In the end** = no final (da história). **At the end** (of) = no fim de algo: at the end of the movie, at the end of the street.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ It was late, because I took a taxi.  
  ✅ **It was late, so I took a taxi.**  
  So = consequência; because = causa.
- ❌ In the final, everything was OK.  
  ✅ **In the end, everything was OK.**  
  "No final" = in the end.
- ❌ Then, after, then, after...  
  ✅ **Then... After that... Later... Finally...**  
  Varie os conectores para a história ficar natural.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Pausas e ênfase ao contar histórias

Bons contadores de histórias fazem **pausas** antes do momento importante e **enfatizam** as palavras-chave: "And then... (pausa) SUDDENLY... the lights went OUT." Grave-se contando e ouça!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L4/pron-01.m4a) And then... suddenly... the lights went out!
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L4/pron-02.m4a) You won't believe what happened!
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L4/pron-03.m4a) Luckily, in the end, everything was fine.

## 📖 Reading — A short story: The last bus [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M3-L4/reading.m4a)

It was a rainy night in London, and Ana was waiting for the last bus. She was tired after a long day at the restaurant. At first, the street was empty. Then an old man arrived. He was carrying a big box. "Is this the bus to Camden?" he asked. "Yes," said Ana. Suddenly, the box moved! The man smiled and opened it: there was a small, wet puppy inside. "I found him in the rain," he said, "but I can't keep him. My apartment doesn't allow dogs." Ana looked at the puppy, and the puppy looked at Ana. In the end, two passengers got off the bus that night: Ana and her new dog, Lucky.

**Questions:**
1. Why was Ana tired?
2. What was in the box?
3. Why couldn't the man keep the puppy?
4. How does the story end?

## 📝 Writing

Write a short story (100–120 words) that starts with "It was a dark and stormy night..." or a true story from your life. Use past simple, past continuous and connectors.

**Model:**

> It was a dark and stormy night. I was home alone, and I was reading a book in bed. Suddenly, the lights went out. At first, I wasn't worried, so I looked for a candle. Then I heard footsteps in the living room! I was terrified, but I took my umbrella — my only weapon — and walked slowly to the door. I opened it and screamed. Luckily, it was only my brother. He forgot his keys, so he came in through the window. In the end, we laughed for hours and ate ice cream in the dark.
