## 🧩 Grammar: Wh- questions no present continuous

Para perguntas com question words, a ordem é:

**Question word + am/is/are + sujeito + verbo-ing?**

- **What** is she **wearing**?
- **Where** are they **going**?
- **Why** is he **laughing**?
- **Who** are you **talking** to?

Para **descrever imagens**, o present continuous é o tempo principal: "A man **is sitting** on a bench. He**'s reading** a book." Combine com **there is / there are**: "**There are** two kids. They**'re playing** soccer."

| Question word | be | Sujeito | Verbo-ing | Resposta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What | is | he | wearing? | A blue hoodie. |
| Where | are | they | sitting? | On the beach. |
| Why | is | she | laughing? | Because the dog is funny. |
| Who | are | you | texting? | My sister. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M2-L2/example-01.m4a) What's the man in the background doing? He's taking a selfie. — _O que o homem ao fundo está fazendo? Está tirando uma selfie._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M2-L2/example-02.m4a) In this photo, my friends are wearing costumes. It's Carnival! — _Nesta foto, meus amigos estão fantasiados. É Carnaval!_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M2-L2/example-03.m4a) There's a girl on the right. She's holding a balloon. — _Tem uma menina à direita. Ela está segurando um balão._

> 💡 **Dica:** Em inglês, **wear** é usado para roupas, acessórios, óculos, perfume e até maquiagem: "She's wearing glasses / perfume / makeup."

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ He is using a blue cap.  
  ✅ **He is wearing a blue cap.**  
  Para roupas e acessórios, o verbo é wear, não use.
- ❌ What she is doing?  
  ✅ **What is she doing?**  
  Na pergunta, o be vem antes do sujeito.
- ❌ a pants / a jeans  
  ✅ **a pair of pants / some jeans**  
  Pants, jeans, shorts e glasses são sempre plurais em inglês.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Wear x where

**Wear** (vestir) e **where** (onde) têm o **mesmo som**: "uér". Os dois soam igual a **ware** (como em software). O contexto resolve!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M2-L2/pron-01.m4a) Where are you going?
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M2-L2/pron-02.m4a) What are you wearing?
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M2-L2/pron-03.m4a) Where's the jacket I wear to work?

## 📖 Reading — Photo challenge: describe it! [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M2-L2/reading.m4a)

This week's photo was taken in a park in London on a sunny Sunday. In the foreground, a family is having a picnic. The mother is pouring juice, and the father is making sandwiches. Their little boy is chasing a dog. In the middle, two teenagers are skateboarding. One is wearing a yellow hoodie, and the other is filming him with a phone. In the background, people are walking and some are lying on the grass. Everyone is enjoying the rare London sun!

**Questions:**
1. What is the family doing?
2. What is the boy doing?
3. What is the teenager with the phone doing?
4. Why is the sun called "rare"?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Descrever imagens em provas

Descrever fotos é uma tarefa clássica de exames como **Cambridge**, **TOEFL** e **Duolingo English Test**. A fórmula: onde é → quem aparece → o que estão fazendo (present continuous) → detalhes de roupas e fundo → sua opinião/hipótese ("They look happy").

## ✅ Resumo
- Question word + be + sujeito + -ing?
- Descrever imagens = present continuous.
- Roupas: wear (nunca use).
- on the left / in the middle / in the background.
