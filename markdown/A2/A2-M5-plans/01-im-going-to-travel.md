## 🧩 Grammar: Going to

**Going to** é a forma mais comum de falar de **planos e intenções** — coisas que você **já decidiu** fazer.

**Estrutura:** sujeito + **am/is/are** + **going to** + verbo base
- I**'m going to** travel to Chile in July.
- She**'s not going to** buy a car.
- **Are** you **going to** study tonight?

Também usamos para **previsões com evidência** (algo que você vê que vai acontecer):
- "Look at those clouds! It**'s going to** rain."
- "Be careful! You**'re going to** fall!"

**Gonna:** na fala informal, "going to" vira **gonna**: "I'm **gonna** call you." Você vai ouvir muito em séries e músicas. Use na fala, evite na escrita formal.

Com **go**, é comum simplificar: "I'm going to go to the beach" → "I'm **going to the beach**."

| Afirmativa | Negativa | Pergunta |
|---|---|---|
| I'm going to travel. | I'm not going to travel. | Am I going to travel? |
| He's going to quit. | He isn't going to quit. | Is he going to quit? |
| We're going to move. | We aren't going to move. | What are you going to do? |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L1/example-01.m4a) We're going to book the hotel tonight. — _Vamos reservar o hotel hoje à noite._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L1/example-02.m4a) I'm going to save money for a backpacking trip. — _Vou economizar para um mochilão._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L1/example-03.m4a) Oh no, the phone is at 1%. It's going to die! — _Ah não, o celular está com 1%. Vai desligar!_

> 💡 **Dica:** Não esqueça o **to be**: "I going to..." ❌ → "I**'m** going to..." ✅. É a mesma "cola" do present continuous.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ I going to travel.  
  ✅ **I'm going to travel.**  
  Going to precisa do verbo to be.
- ❌ She's going to traveling.  
  ✅ **She's going to travel.**  
  Depois de going to, use o verbo na forma base.
- ❌ I will to visit my mom.  
  ✅ **I'm going to visit my mom. / I will visit my mom.**  
  Will não leva to.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Gonna

**Going to** + verbo vira **gonna** ("gâna") na fala: "I'm gonna go" = "áim gâna gôu". Atenção: só vira gonna quando é futuro. "I'm going **to** the gym" (estou indo para) **não** vira "gonna the gym"!

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L1/pron-01.m4a) I'm gonna call you later.

## 📖 Reading — Gap year plans [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L1/reading.m4a)

Lucas is 18 and he just finished high school. He isn't going to start college this year — he's going to take a gap year. "First, I'm going to work in a restaurant for four months to save money," he says. "Then I'm going to travel around South America by bus. I'm going to volunteer at an animal shelter in Bolivia for a month." His parents were worried at first. "But he has a plan and a budget," says his mom. "And he's going to start college next year. I think it's going to be a great experience."

**Questions:**
1. Is Lucas going to start college this year?
2. Why is he going to work in a restaurant?
3. What is he going to do in Bolivia?
4. Why is his mom less worried now?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Gap year

O **gap year** — um ano sabático entre a escola e a faculdade — é comum no Reino Unido, na Austrália e cada vez mais nos EUA. Muitos jovens trabalham, viajam ou fazem voluntariado. No Brasil, a ideia está crescendo, principalmente com intercâmbios de trabalho.
