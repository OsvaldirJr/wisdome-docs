## 🧩 Grammar: Will (e comparação com going to)

**Estrutura:** **will** + verbo base (igual para todas as pessoas, sem -s, sem to)
- Afirmativa: I**'ll** help. · Negativa: I **won't** (= will not) forget. · Pergunta: **Will** you be there?

**Usos de will:**
1. **Decisão na hora** (não planejada): "The phone is ringing!" → "I**'ll** get it!"
2. **Ofertas:** "Your bag looks heavy. I**'ll** carry it."
3. **Promessas:** "I **won't** be late, I promise."
4. **Pedidos em restaurante:** "I**'ll** have the fish."
5. **Previsões/opiniões** (sem evidência concreta): "I **think** robots **will** do most jobs." / "She**'ll probably** love it."

**Will x going to:**
- **going to** = já decidido antes: "I**'m going to** buy a car. I saved money for a year."
- **will** = decidido agora: "Oh, this car is on sale? OK, I**'ll** buy it!"

**Shall I...?** = Quer que eu...? (oferta, mais britânico): "**Shall I** call a taxi?"

| Forma | Uso | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| going to | plano já decidido / evidência | I'm going to study medicine. |
| present continuous | compromisso marcado | I'm meeting Ana at 5. |
| will | decisão na hora | I'm cold. — I'll close the window. |
| will | oferta / promessa | I'll pay you back tomorrow. |
| will | previsão / opinião | I think it will be a great year. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L4/example-01.m4a) "We don't have milk." "Don't worry, I'll buy some." — _"Não temos leite." "Não se preocupa, eu compro."_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L4/example-02.m4a) I promise I won't tell anyone. — _Prometo que não vou contar para ninguém._
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L4/example-03.m4a) I think self-driving cars will be common in 2040. — _Acho que carros autônomos serão comuns em 2040._

> 💡 **Dica:** **Won't** (will not) soa "uônt" — diferente de **want** ("uónt", querer). "I won't go" = não vou · "I want to go" = quero ir. Cuidado!

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ I will to call you.  
  ✅ **I'll call you.**  
  Will + verbo base, sem to.
- ❌ She wills come.  
  ✅ **She'll come.**  
  Will não varia: sem -s.
- ❌ "The phone is ringing!" "I'm going to answer it."  
  ✅ **"I'll answer it!"**  
  Decisão na hora = will.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: 'll e won't

A contração **'ll** é um L escuro rápido, quase só a língua no céu da boca: **I'll** = "áil", **you'll** = "iúl", **it'll** = "íról". E **won't** ("uônt", com o "ô" fechado) ≠ **want** ("uónt").

- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L4/pron-01.m4a) I'll help you.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L4/pron-02.m4a) It'll be fine.
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L4/pron-03.m4a) I won't forget. / I want to go.

## 📖 Reading — Predictions from the past [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L4/reading.m4a)

In 1900, an American engineer made predictions for the year 2000. Some were right: "People will talk to friends in other countries by telephone," and "Photos will arrive from far away in seconds." Others were wrong: "There will be no letters C, X or Q in the alphabet," and "Mosquitoes and flies will disappear." Today, experts make new predictions: we'll live longer, cities will be greener, and AI will be part of everything. Will they be right? We'll see!

**Questions:**
1. When were the first predictions made?
2. Give one prediction that was right.
3. Give one prediction that was wrong.
4. What do experts predict today?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "I'll call you" e outras promessas vazias

Assim como o brasileiro diz "vamos marcar!", em inglês há promessas que nem sempre são literais: **"I'll call you!"**, **"We should hang out sometime!"**, **"Let's do lunch!"**. Se a pessoa não marca dia e hora, provavelmente é só educação. 😉

## ✅ Resumo
- will + verbo base (I'll / won't).
- Decisões na hora, ofertas, promessas, pedidos, previsões.
- going to = plano · present continuous = agendado · will = agora/opinião.
- won't (não vai) ≠ want (querer).
