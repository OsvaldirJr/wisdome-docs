# Review — Plans
**A2-M5** — Revise as formas de futuro: going to, present continuous, will e convites.

## O que você aprendeu neste módulo
- Falar sobre planos e intenções com going to
- Combinar compromissos com o present continuous
- Fazer, aceitar e recusar convites com educação
- Usar will para decisões rápidas, ofertas e promessas
- Escolher a forma de futuro adequada a cada situação

## ✏️ Review exercises

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct option

_Choose the correct option._

1. Look at the clouds. It ___ rain.  
   ( ) will · ( ) is going to · ( ) is raining
2. What ___ on Saturday night?  
   ( ) do you do · ( ) are you doing · ( ) will you to do
3. "I'm hungry." "I ___ make you a sandwich."  
   ( ) 'll · ( ) 'm going to · ( ) 'm making
4. Would you like ___ with us?  
   ( ) come · ( ) to come · ( ) coming
5. How about ___ a pizza?  
   ( ) order · ( ) to order · ( ) ordering

### Exercise 2 — Future forms

_Complete with the best future form._

1. We ___ (fly) to Rome on May 3rd. We have the tickets.
2. I ___ (start) a diet next month. I decided yesterday.
3. Don't worry, I ___ (not / tell) anyone.

## 🎤 Free conversation

Grave um áudio de 2 minutos falando sobre seu futuro: seus compromissos da semana, seus planos para o ano e suas previsões para daqui a 10 anos.

1. What are you doing this week?
2. What are you going to do next year?
3. What do you think your life will be like in 10 years?
4. Invite me to something! (I'll accept or refuse.)
5. What's one promise you want to make to yourself?

## 🚀 Mini-projeto

**Plan a trip:** em grupo, planeje uma viagem de fim de semana em inglês: destino, transporte, hospedagem e roteiro dia a dia (going to), horários marcados (present continuous / simple present) e previsões do que vai acontecer (will). Apresente o plano em 2 minutos.
