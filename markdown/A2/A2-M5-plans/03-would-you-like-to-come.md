## 🧩 Grammar: Convites e sugestões

**Convidar:**
- **Would you like to** + verbo? (educado, neutro) → "Would you like to come to dinner?"
- **Do you want to** + verbo? (informal, entre amigos) → "Do you wanna watch a movie?"

**Would like x like:**
- **like** = gostar (em geral): "I **like** coffee."
- **would like** = gostaria/quero (agora, educado): "I**'d like** a coffee, please."

**Sugerir:**
- **Let's** + verbo → "**Let's** order pizza!"
- **How about / What about** + verbo-**ing** → "**How about going** to the movies?"
- **Why don't we** + verbo? → "**Why don't we** try the new restaurant?"

**Responder:**
✅ Aceitar: "I'd love to!" / "Sounds great!" / "Count me in!" / "Sure, why not?"
❌ Recusar com educação: **agradecer + desculpa + motivo + (alternativa)**:
"**Thanks** for the invite! **I'm afraid I can't** — **I'm working** that night. **Maybe another time?**"

| Função | Estrutura | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Convite (educado) | Would you like to + verbo? | Would you like to join us? |
| Convite (informal) | Do you want to + verbo? | Do you want to hang out? |
| Sugestão | Let's + verbo | Let's take a break. |
| Sugestão | How about + -ing? | How about having a BBQ? |
| Sugestão | Why don't we + verbo? | Why don't we stay in? |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L3/example-01.m4a) Would you like to come to my housewarming party on Saturday? — _Você gostaria de vir à minha festa de casa nova no sábado?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L3/example-02.m4a) Thanks so much! I'd love to, but I'm visiting my parents. Can I take a rain check? — _Muito obrigado! Eu adoraria, mas vou visitar meus pais. Fica para a próxima?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L3/example-03.m4a) How about meeting at the park instead? — _Que tal nos encontrarmos no parque, então?_

> 💡 **Dica:** Uma resposta só com **"No."** soa muito rude em inglês. Sempre agradeça e dê um motivo, mesmo que curto.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Would you like come?  
  ✅ **Would you like to come?**  
  Would like + to + verbo.
- ❌ How about go to the beach?  
  ✅ **How about going to the beach?**  
  How about + verbo-ing.
- ❌ Let's to go!  
  ✅ **Let's go!**  
  Let's + verbo base, sem to.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Would you = "wúdja"

**Would you** se junta na fala: "**wúdja**". **Do you want to** vira "**dja wâna**" ou até "**wâna**": "Wanna grab a coffee?" E **I'd** soa "áid" — não esqueça o D!

## 📖 Reading — The art of saying no [🔊](../../../audio/A2/A2-M5-L3/reading.m4a)

Many people find it hard to say no to invitations. They're afraid of being rude. But experts say that a polite "no" is better than a "yes" you regret. Here's a simple formula: 1. Thank the person: "Thanks so much for inviting me!" 2. Say no clearly: "I'm afraid I can't make it." 3. Give a short reason (optional): "I need a quiet weekend." 4. Offer an alternative if you want: "How about coffee next week?" And remember: "Maybe" is not a polite answer if you mean "no" — it only creates expectations!

**Questions:**
1. Why do people find it hard to say no?
2. What's the first step of the formula?
3. Is the reason obligatory?
4. Why is "maybe" not a good answer?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — RSVP e "potluck"

Em convites formais aparece **RSVP** (do francês "répondez s'il vous plaît" — responda, por favor): o anfitrião precisa saber se você vai. E um **potluck** é uma festa em que cada convidado leva um prato — como o nosso "cada um leva alguma coisa".
