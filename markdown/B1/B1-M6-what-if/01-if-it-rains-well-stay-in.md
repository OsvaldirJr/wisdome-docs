## 🧩 Grammar: Zero and first conditional

**Zero conditional** → fatos, verdades gerais, coisas que **sempre** acontecem:
**If + present simple, present simple**
- "If you **heat** water to 100°C, it **boils**."
- "If I **drink** coffee after 6 p.m., I **can't** sleep."
(Aqui, **if** = **when**: sempre que.)

**First conditional** → situações **reais e possíveis no futuro** e suas consequências:
**If + present simple, will + verbo**
- "If it **rains** tomorrow, we**'ll stay** home."
- "If you **don't hurry**, you**'ll miss** the bus."
- "What **will** you **do** if you **don't get** the job?"

⚠️ **Regra de ouro:** depois de **if**, **nunca** use will: ❌ "If it will rain..." ✅ "If it **rains**..." (em português usamos o futuro do subjuntivo — "se chover" — mas em inglês é presente simples).

A ordem pode inverter (sem vírgula): "We'll stay home **if** it rains."

**Variações comuns na segunda parte:**
- modal: "If you finish early, you **can** go home." / "If it's sunny, we **might** go to the beach."
- imperativo: "If you see Ana, **tell** her to call me."
- going to: "If he's late again, I**'m going to** talk to him."

| Tipo | If-clause | Main clause | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero | present simple | present simple | If you mix blue and yellow, you get green. |
| First | present simple | will + verbo | If you study, you'll pass. |
| First (modal) | present simple | can / might / should | If it's sunny, we might go out. |
| First (imperativo) | present simple | imperativo | If you need anything, call me. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L1/example-01.m4a) If we leave now, we'll get there before the traffic. — _Se sairmos agora, vamos chegar antes do trânsito._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L1/example-02.m4a) If plants don't get enough light, they die. — _Se as plantas não recebem luz suficiente, elas morrem._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L1/example-03.m4a) If the flight is cancelled, you'll get a refund. — _Se o voo for cancelado, você vai receber reembolso._

> 💡 **Dica:** O mesmo vale para **when**, **as soon as**, **before**, **after**, **until** falando do futuro: "I'll call you **when** I **arrive**" (❌ "when I will arrive"). Você vai ver mais na lição 3.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ If it will rain, we will stay home.  
  ✅ **If it rains, we'll stay home.**  
  Depois de if, use o presente simples (não will).
- ❌ If you will need help, call me.  
  ✅ **If you need help, call me.**  
  Depois de if, presente simples.
- ❌ If I'll have time, I'll go.  
  ✅ **If I have time, I'll go.**  
  O will vai só na outra parte da frase.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: 'll fraco e entonação do if

Na frase condicional, a voz **sobe** no fim da parte com if e **desce** no fim da consequência: "If it rains ↗, we'll stay home ↘." O **'ll** é rápido: "we'll" = "uíl", "you'll" = "iúl", "it'll" = "íról".

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L1/pron-01.m4a) If it rains ↗, we'll stay in ↘.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L1/pron-02.m4a) If you need help ↗, call me ↘.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L1/pron-03.m4a) You'll miss the bus if you don't hurry.

## 📖 Reading — Small habits, big results [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L1/reading.m4a)

If you read ten pages a day, you'll read about a dozen books a year. If you save ten reais a day, you'll have more than 3,600 reais in twelve months. This is the logic behind "small habits", an idea popularized by writers like James Clear. The concept is simple: small actions, repeated consistently, produce big results over time. It works the other way too: if you sleep one hour less every night, you'll lose more than 300 hours of sleep in a year — and your health will suffer. Experts suggest using "if-then" plans to build new habits: "If it's 7 a.m., I'll drink a glass of water." "If I feel like checking social media, I'll do five push-ups first." Studies show that people who make if-then plans are much more likely to achieve their goals.

**Questions:**
1. What will happen if you read ten pages a day?
2. What's the concept of small habits?
3. What will happen if you sleep one hour less every night?
4. What are if-then plans?

## 📝 Writing

Write 6 "if-then" plans (zero or first conditional) to build a new habit or reach a goal, then a short paragraph (60–80 words) explaining your plan B if things go wrong.

**Model:**

> Goal: run a 10K in six months.  
> 1. If it's Monday, Wednesday or Friday, I'll run after work.  
> 2. If it rains, I'll do a home workout video.  
> 3. If I feel tired, I'll walk instead of running — but I won't skip.  
> 4. If I run 3 times in a week, I'll treat myself to açaí.  
> 5. If I don't sleep 7 hours, I get injured more easily, so I'll go to bed at 10:30.  
> 6. If I lose motivation, I'll call my running buddy.  
> Plan B: If I get injured, I'll stop running and see a physiotherapist. If the injury is serious, I'll choose a later race and focus on swimming, which is easier on the knees. If I can't do the 10K in six months, I'll do a 5K first. The important thing is not to give up.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "Just in case" e o "plano B"

Em inglês, ter um **backup plan** é sinal de profissionalismo. Expressões úteis: **"What if...?"** (E se...?), **"Let's hope for the best and prepare for the worst"** e **"Better safe than sorry"** (melhor prevenir do que remediar).

## ✅ Resumo
- Zero: If + present, present (fatos gerais).
- First: If + present, will + verbo (futuro real).
- Nunca will depois de if.
- Pode usar modal ou imperativo na consequência.
