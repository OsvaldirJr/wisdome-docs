## 🧩 Grammar: Unless e time clauses no futuro

**Unless** = **if... not** (a menos que):
- "**Unless** you study, you'll fail." = "**If** you **don't** study, you'll fail."
- ⚠️ Unless já é negativo: ❌ "Unless you don't study..."

**Time clauses no futuro** — a mesma regra do if: depois de **when, as soon as, before, after, until**, use o **presente simples**, mesmo falando do futuro:
- "I'll call you **when** I **arrive**." (❌ when I will arrive)
- "**As soon as** I **get** the results, I'll let you know."
- "We'll wait **until** the rain **stops**."
- "**Before** you **leave**, turn off the lights."
- "**After** I **finish** this course, I'm going to take the TOEFL."

Também dá para usar o **present perfect** para enfatizar que a ação estará completa: "I'll call you **as soon as** I**'ve finished**."

**In case** = por precaução (caso aconteça):
- "Take an umbrella **in case** it rains." (leve — talvez chova)
- Compare: "Take an umbrella **if** it rains." (só leve se estiver chovendo)

**As long as / provided that** = desde que (condição):
- "You can use my car **as long as** you **drive** carefully."

| Conector | Sentido | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| unless | se não, a menos que | I won't go unless you come too. |
| as soon as | assim que | I'll text you as soon as I arrive. |
| until | até que | Stay here until I call you. |
| when | quando | When I retire, I'll live by the sea. |
| in case | por precaução | Save the file in case the power goes out. |
| as long as | desde que | You can go as long as you're back by 10. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L3/example-01.m4a) Unless something unexpected happens, I'll finish my degree next year. — _A menos que aconteça algo inesperado, termino a faculdade ano que vem._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L3/example-02.m4a) As soon as I reach B2, I'm going to apply for jobs abroad. — _Assim que eu chegar ao B2, vou me candidatar a vagas no exterior._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L3/example-03.m4a) Write your goals down in case you forget why you started. — _Anote suas metas caso você esqueça por que começou._

> 💡 **Dica:** **Until** = até (tempo). **By** = até no máximo (prazo): "Wait **until** 5" (espere até as 5) x "Send it **by** 5" (envie até as 5, no máximo).

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Unless you don't hurry, you'll be late.  
  ✅ **Unless you hurry, you'll be late.**  
  Unless já significa "se não". Não use duas negativas.
- ❌ I'll call you when I will arrive.  
  ✅ **I'll call you when I arrive.**  
  Depois de when/as soon as, presente simples.
- ❌ Send me the report until Friday.  
  ✅ **Send me the report by Friday.**  
  Prazo = by. Until = duração até um ponto.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Unless, until, as soon as

**Unless** = "an-LÉS" (força no fim). **Until** = "an-TÍL" (força no fim). **As soon as** é dito rápido, como uma palavra: "azsúnaz".

## 📖 Reading — SMART goals [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M6-L3/reading.m4a)

"I want to improve my English" is a wish, not a goal. Unless a goal is specific, it's very hard to achieve. That's why many coaches recommend SMART goals: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound. Compare "I'll study more" with "I'll study English for 30 minutes a day, five days a week, until I reach B2 by December." The second one tells you exactly what to do, how to measure it and when the deadline is. Experts also suggest breaking big goals into milestones. As soon as you reach one, celebrate — and then move to the next. Keep track of your progress in a notebook or app, in case you lose motivation on difficult days: looking back at how far you've come is one of the best ways to keep going.

**Questions:**
1. What's the difference between a wish and a goal?
2. What does SMART stand for?
3. What should you do as soon as you reach a milestone?
4. Why should you keep track of your progress?

## 📝 Writing

Write your personal development plan (120–150 words): one main SMART goal, three milestones with deadlines and what you'll do if things go wrong. Use at least 5 connectors from this lesson.

**Model:**

> My SMART goal: reach B2 in English by next December and pass an international exam.  
>   
> Milestone 1: By March, I'll finish the B1 course and watch one series episode in English every day without subtitles in Portuguese.  
> Milestone 2: As soon as I finish B1, I'll join a conversation club and speak English for at least two hours a week until I feel confident.  
> Milestone 3: In September, I'll take a practice exam. Unless my score is good, I won't book the real exam — I'll study for two more months instead.  
>

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Deadlines e "ASAP"

No trabalho em inglês, você vai ver **ASAP** (as soon as possible — o mais rápido possível), **EOD** (end of day — até o fim do dia) e **COB** (close of business — fim do expediente). "Please send it **by EOD**" = mande até o fim do dia.

- in case = por precaução · as long as = desde que.
- until = até (duração) · by = até no máximo (prazo).
