## 🧩 Grammar: Pedidos e How could you...?

**Escala de educação dos pedidos:**
1. **Can you** + verbo? → informal, amigos e família
2. **Could you** + verbo? → educado, o mais versátil
3. **Would you** + verbo? → educado
4. **Would you mind** + verbo-**ing**? → muito educado (bom para favores grandes)
5. **I was wondering if you could** + verbo → muito indireto e formal

Para pedidos grandes, **prepare o terreno**: "Could you do me a **big** favor?" / "I hate to ask, but..."

**Respostas:**
- Sim: Sure! / Of course. / No problem. / I'd be happy to.
- Não (com educação): I'm sorry, I can't. I... / I'd love to help, but... / I'm afraid I'm busy.

**How could you...?** = Como você pôde...? (indignação ou surpresa no passado)
"**How could you** leave your keys at home **again**?!"

**Why couldn't you...?** = Por que você não conseguiu / não pôde...?
"**Why couldn't you** call me earlier?"

| Nível | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| Informal | Can you pass the salt? |
| Educado | Could you close the window, please? |
| Educado | Would you help me with this box? |
| Muito educado | Would you mind watering my plants? |
| Formal / indireto | I was wondering if you could review my CV. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M3-L2/example-01.m4a) Could you do me a favor? Could you feed my cat this weekend? — _Você poderia me fazer um favor? Poderia alimentar meu gato neste fim de semana?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M3-L2/example-02.m4a) I was wondering if you could write me a recommendation letter. — _Eu queria saber se você poderia me escrever uma carta de recomendação._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M3-L2/example-03.m4a) How could you lock yourself out of the house again? — _Como você conseguiu se trancar do lado de fora de novo?_

> 💡 **Dica:** Depois de um favor, diga **"I owe you one!"** (te devo uma) ou **"You're a lifesaver!"** (você salvou minha vida). E para responder a um agradecimento: **"Anytime!"** (quando precisar).

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Could you to help me?  
  ✅ **Could you help me?**  
  Could + verbo base.
- ❌ Would you mind to pick me up?  
  ✅ **Would you mind picking me up?**  
  Would you mind + -ing.
- ❌ Can you make me a favor?  
  ✅ **Can you do me a favor?**  
  Fazer um favor = do a favor (não make).

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Entonação de pedidos

Um pedido com entonação **subindo** e voz suave soa educado: "Could you help me? ↗". A mesma frase com entonação descendo e voz seca soa como ordem. E **How could you...?!** tem ênfase forte no **could**: "How **COULD** you?!"

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M3-L2/pron-01.m4a) Could you do me a favor? ↗
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M3-L2/pron-02.m4a) Would you mind picking me up? ↗
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M3-L2/pron-03.m4a) How COULD you forget?!

## 📖 Reading — The Ben Franklin effect [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M3-L2/reading.m4a)

Do you want someone to like you more? Ask them for a favor. It sounds strange, but it's a well-known psychological phenomenon called the Ben Franklin effect. In the 18th century, Benjamin Franklin had a political rival who didn't like him. Instead of being extra nice to him, Franklin wrote a polite letter asking if he could borrow a rare book from the man's library. The rival lent it, Franklin returned it with a warm thank-you note, and the two became friends. Psychologists explain that when we do someone a favor, our brain looks for a reason: "I helped this person, so I must like them." The lesson? Small favors — "Could you recommend a good restaurant?" — can build relationships. Just don't ask for too many!

**Questions:**
1. What is the Ben Franklin effect?
2. What did Franklin ask his rival?
3. How do psychologists explain the effect?
4. What is the warning at the end?

## 📝 Writing

Write two messages asking for a favor (60–80 words each): one informal to a friend, one formal to a teacher or manager.

**Model:**

> Informal:  
> Hey Leo! Can you do me a huge favor? My car broke down and I need to pick up my mom at the airport on Saturday at 10. Could you drive me there? I'll buy you lunch after — promise! I owe you one. 🙏  
>   
> Formal:  
> Dear Professor Martins,  
> I hope you are well. I'm applying for a master's program in Portugal, and I was wondering if you could write me a letter of recommendation. The deadline is May 30th. I would be happy to send you my CV and any information you need.  
> Thank you very much for considering my request.  
> Best regards,  
> Ana Souza

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Pedir ajuda no trabalho

Em muitas empresas de cultura anglo-saxã, pedir ajuda é visto como sinal de maturidade, não de fraqueza. Frases úteis: **"Do you have a minute?"**, **"Could I pick your brain about something?"** (posso aproveitar seu conhecimento?) e **"Could you walk me through this?"** (pode me explicar passo a passo?).
