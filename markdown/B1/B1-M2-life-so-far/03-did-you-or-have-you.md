## 🧩 Grammar: Present perfect x simple past

**A pergunta-chave:** o tempo está **terminado** ou **ainda aberto**?

**Simple past** → tempo **terminado e definido**. Você sabe (ou diz) **quando**.
- Palavras: yesterday, last week, in 2020, two days ago, when I was a child, on Monday, What time...? When...?
- "I **visited** Rome **in 2019**." / "**When did** you **arrive**?"

**Present perfect** → tempo **aberto** ou **não mencionado**; o foco é o **resultado agora** ou a **experiência**.
- Palavras: ever, never, before, already, yet, just, so far, recently, lately, for, since, How long...?
- "I**'ve visited** Rome **three times**." / "**Have** you **ever been** to Rome?"

**Períodos ainda abertos** (today, this week, this month, this year) usam **present perfect** se o período não acabou:
- "I**'ve had** three coffees **today**." (o dia não acabou)
- "I **had** three coffees **yesterday**." (acabou)

**Notícias:** a primeira frase anuncia com present perfect; os detalhes vêm no simple past:
"Scientists **have discovered** a new planet. They **found** it last month using a new telescope."

| Simple past (terminado) | Present perfect (aberto / sem tempo) |
|---|---|
| I saw that movie last week. | I've seen that movie (twice). |
| She lived in Tokyo for 2 years. (não mora mais) | She's lived in Tokyo for 2 years. (ainda mora) |
| Did you finish the report yesterday? | Have you finished the report yet? |
| I wrote three emails this morning. (já é tarde) | I've written three emails this morning. (ainda é manhã) |
| When did you meet him? | How long have you known him? |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M2-L3/example-01.m4a) Have you heard? Brazil has won the gold medal! They beat Japan in the final last night. — _Ficou sabendo? O Brasil ganhou a medalha de ouro! Venceu o Japão na final ontem à noite._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M2-L3/example-02.m4a) I've been to Chile, but I didn't go to the Atacama Desert. — _Já fui ao Chile, mas não fui ao Deserto do Atacama._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M2-L3/example-03.m4a) We've had a great year so far. Sales grew 20% in the first quarter. — _Tivemos um ótimo ano até agora. As vendas cresceram 20% no primeiro trimestre._

> 💡 **Dica:** Atalho mental: se dá para responder **"When?"** com uma data ou momento, é **simple past**. Se a resposta é **"Not important"** ou **"Until now"**, é **present perfect**.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ I've bought this phone two years ago.  
  ✅ **I bought this phone two years ago.**  
  Ago indica tempo definido → simple past.
- ❌ When have you arrived?  
  ✅ **When did you arrive?**  
  When pede um momento definido → simple past.
- ❌ Did you ever try açaí?  
  ✅ **Have you ever tried açaí?**  
  Experiência de vida, sem tempo definido → present perfect (padrão).

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Ouvir a diferença: I've / I

Na fala rápida, "I've seen" e "I saw" soam bem diferentes, mas "I've finished" e "I finished" podem ser quase iguais — o 've é só um "v" leve. Preste atenção ao **contexto** (palavras como yet, already, yesterday) para entender o tempo.

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M2-L3/pron-01.m4a) I've finished. / I finished yesterday.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M2-L3/pron-02.m4a) Have you heard the news?
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M2-L3/pron-03.m4a) When did it happen?

## 📖 Reading — My first year abroad [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M2-L3/reading.m4a)

It's been exactly one year since I moved to Montreal, and what a year it's been! When I arrived last September, I didn't speak any French, and my English was basic. I lived in a tiny room and worked in a restaurant kitchen. Since then, a lot has changed. I've taken two French courses, and I've made friends from more than ten countries. I've also found a job in my area — I started as a junior architect in March. Of course, it hasn't all been easy. I've missed my family terribly, and I'll never forget my first winter: in January, the temperature fell to minus 30 degrees, and I cried on my way to work. But so far, moving here has been the best decision of my life.

**Questions:**
1. When did the writer move to Montreal?
2. What has the writer done since then?
3. When did the writer start the new job?
4. What happened in January?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Manchetes em inglês

Manchetes de jornal usam um "inglês telegráfico": **presente simples para o passado** ("Teacher **wins** award"), sem artigos e sem verbo to be ("Mayor **to visit** school" = o prefeito vai visitar). Mas o primeiro parágrafo da notícia segue a regra: present perfect + simple past.

## ✅ Resumo
- Tempo terminado/definido → simple past.
- Tempo aberto ou não mencionado → present perfect.
- ago, last, yesterday, when, in 2020 → simple past.
- ever, never, yet, already, just, so far, since → present perfect.
