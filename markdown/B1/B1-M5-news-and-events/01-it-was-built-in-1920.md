## 🧩 Grammar: Passive voice: be + past participle

Na **voz ativa**, o foco está em **quem faz**: "Oscar Niemeyer **designed** Brasília."
Na **voz passiva**, o foco está **no que aconteceu** ou **no objeto**: "Brasília **was designed** by Oscar Niemeyer."

**Estrutura:** sujeito + **be** (no tempo certo) + **particípio passado** (+ by + agente)

- **Present simple passive:** am / is / are + particípio → "Coffee **is grown** in Brazil." / "These phones **are made** in China."
- **Past simple passive:** was / were + particípio → "The Eiffel Tower **was built** in 1889." / "The windows **were broken**."

**Negativa e pergunta:** mexa só no **be**: "It **wasn't made** in Italy." / "**Where was** it **made**?" / "**When were** the pyramids **built**?"

**Quando usar a passiva?**
1. Quando **não sabemos** quem fez: "My bike **was stolen**."
2. Quando **não importa** quem fez: "The office **is cleaned** every night."
3. Quando o **óbvio**: "He **was arrested**." (pela polícia, claro)
4. Em textos **formais, científicos e notícias**.

**By + agente** só quando a informação é importante: "*Dom Casmurro* **was written by** Machado de Assis."

| Ativa | Passiva |
|---|---|
| People speak Portuguese in Brazil. | Portuguese is spoken in Brazil. |
| They make these shoes in Franca. | These shoes are made in Franca. |
| Santos Dumont flew the 14-bis in 1906. | The 14-bis was flown by Santos Dumont in 1906. |
| Someone stole my wallet. | My wallet was stolen. |
| They didn't invite us. | We weren't invited. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L1/example-01.m4a) Christ the Redeemer was completed in 1931 and was chosen as one of the New Seven Wonders in 2007. — _O Cristo Redentor foi concluído em 1931 e foi escolhido como uma das Novas Sete Maravilhas em 2007._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L1/example-02.m4a) Most of the world's açaí is produced in the state of Pará. — _A maior parte do açaí do mundo é produzida no estado do Pará._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L1/example-03.m4a) Was the meeting cancelled? — Yes, it was moved to Monday. — _A reunião foi cancelada? — Sim, foi remarcada para segunda._

> 💡 **Dica:** Para a passiva funcionar, você precisa dominar os **particípios** (built, made, written, sold, spoken, grown, stolen, chosen). Revise a lista de verbos irregulares!

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ This house built in 1950.  
  ✅ **This house was built in 1950.**  
  A passiva precisa do verbo be.
- ❌ The book was wrote by Clarice Lispector.  
  ✅ **The book was written by Clarice Lispector.**  
  Use o particípio (written), não o passado (wrote).
- ❌ It was invented for Santos Dumont.  
  ✅ **It was invented by Santos Dumont.**  
  O agente da passiva é introduzido por by.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Was e were fracos na passiva

Na passiva, **was / were / is** são fracos e rápidos; a força vai no particípio: "It was **BUILT** in 1931." ("it uâz bilt"). **Built** = "bilt" (o U é mudo!).

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L1/pron-01.m4a) It was BUILT in 1931.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L1/pron-02.m4a) Coffee is GROWN in Minas.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L1/pron-03.m4a) My bike was STOLEN!

## 📖 Reading — The story of coffee [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L1/reading.m4a)

Coffee is drunk by billions of people every day, but where did it come from? According to legend, coffee was discovered in Ethiopia, when a goat herder noticed that his goats became very energetic after eating some red berries. The plant was later taken to Yemen, where coffee was first prepared as a drink. In the 18th century, coffee seeds were brought to Brazil — legend says they were secretly given to a Portuguese officer in French Guiana. Today, Brazil is the world's largest coffee producer: around a third of the world's coffee is grown here, mainly in Minas Gerais, São Paulo and Espírito Santo. The beans are harvested between May and September and are exported to more than 100 countries.

**Questions:**
1. Where was coffee discovered, according to legend?
2. Where was coffee first prepared as a drink?
3. How much of the world's coffee is grown in Brazil?
4. When are the beans harvested?

Write a short description (100–120 words) of a landmark, product or tradition from your city or region. Use at least 6 passive forms.

**Model:**

> Cheese bread (pão de queijo) is one of Brazil's most famous snacks. It's believed that the recipe was created in Minas Gerais in the 18th century, when wheat flour was hard to find. Instead, cassava starch was used, which is why the bread is naturally gluten-free. Traditionally, the dough is made with cassava starch, eggs, milk, oil and Minas cheese. Today, pão de queijo is eaten all over Brazil, usually with coffee in the afternoon, and frozen versions are exported to many countries. In Minas, it's said that every family has its own secret recipe — and the best one is always grandma's!

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "Made in..."

O rótulo **"Made in China"** é famoso, mas hoje é comum ver **"Designed in California, assembled in China"** (projetado na Califórnia, montado na China). Repare: tudo na passiva! A cadeia global de produção é um ótimo tema para praticar.

## ✅ Resumo
- be + particípio = passiva.
- is/are + particípio (presente) · was/were + particípio (passado).
- by + agente só quando importa.
- Use quando o agente é desconhecido, óbvio ou sem importância.
