## 🔥 Warm-up
"Quando cheguei ao cinema, o filme **já tinha começado**." Esse "já tinha começado" é o **past perfect**: uma ação passada que aconteceu **antes** de outra ação passada.

- Have you ever arrived somewhere and something had already happened?
- Had you studied English before this course?

## 🧩 Grammar: Past perfect

**Estrutura:** **had** + particípio passado (igual para todas as pessoas)
- Afirmativa: I **had** (I'd) **finished**. / She'd **left**.
- Negativa: They **hadn't seen** it.
- Pergunta: **Had** you **been** there before?

**Uso:** mostrar que uma ação aconteceu **antes** de outra ação **no passado**. É o "tinha feito".

Linha do tempo:
[ação 1 — past perfect] → [ação 2 — simple past] → agora
"When I **got** to the station, the train **had left**." (o trem saiu primeiro; depois eu cheguei)

Compare:
- "When I arrived, the train **left**." = cheguei e então o trem saiu (vi saindo)
- "When I arrived, the train **had left**." = o trem saiu antes de eu chegar (perdi!)

**Palavras frequentes:** **by the time**, **when**, **already**, **just**, **never... before**, **before**, **after**, **because**.

**Já com before e after**, a ordem fica clara e o past perfect é opcional: "After I (had) finished, I went home."

💡 Em notícias e histórias, o past perfect volta no tempo para explicar o contexto: "The firefighters rescued a man who **had been** trapped for six hours."

| Primeira ação (past perfect) | Segunda ação (simple past) |
|---|---|
| The movie had started | when we arrived. |
| She had never flown | before she went to Japan. |
| By the time help came, | they had walked 20 km. |
| I had just fallen asleep | when the phone rang. |
| He was nervous because | he hadn't prepared. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L2/example-01.m4a) By the time the firefighters arrived, the neighbors had already rescued the dog. — _Quando os bombeiros chegaram, os vizinhos já tinham resgatado o cachorro._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L2/example-02.m4a) I'd never seen snow before I moved to Canada. — _Eu nunca tinha visto neve antes de me mudar para o Canadá._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L2/example-03.m4a) It turned out that the man had lost his memory after the accident. — _Descobriu-se que o homem tinha perdido a memória depois do acidente._

> 💡 **Dica:** Cuidado com a contração **'d**: pode ser **had** ("I'd finished") ou **would** ("I'd go"). Se vem particípio depois, é **had**; se vem verbo base, é **would**.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ When I arrived, the party already finished.  
  ✅ **When I arrived, the party had already finished.**  
  A festa terminou antes da chegada: past perfect.
- ❌ I had saw that movie before.  
  ✅ **I had seen that movie before.**  
  Past perfect usa o particípio (seen).
- ❌ I had went to bed when you called.  
  ✅ **I had gone to bed when you called.**  
  Particípio de go = gone.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: I'd / she'd / they'd

Na fala, **had** quase sempre vira **'d**, que é muito curto: "I'd left" = "áid léft", "she'd gone" = "shíd gón". Às vezes o 'd é quase inaudível — o particípio é o que mostra que é past perfect.

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L2/pron-01.m4a) The train had left.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L2/pron-02.m4a) I'd never seen it before.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L2/pron-03.m4a) By the time we arrived, they'd gone.

## 📖 Reading — The wallet that came back [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M5-L2/reading.m4a)

Last month, a taxi driver in Belo Horizonte made headlines for an act of honesty. Carlos Mendes, 54, had just finished his shift when he noticed a wallet on the back seat. A passenger had dropped it earlier that day. Inside, there was 3,000 reais in cash, credit cards and a plane ticket to Lisbon for the next morning. Carlos didn't have the owner's phone number, but he found a business card in the wallet. He called the number, and it turned out that it belonged to the passenger's daughter. By the time Carlos reached the passenger's house, the man had already canceled his cards and had almost given up on his trip. "I'd never felt so relieved," said the passenger. When he tried to give Carlos a reward, the driver refused. "I only did what anyone should do," he said.

**Questions:**
1. When did Carlos notice the wallet?
2. What was in the wallet?
3. How did Carlos find the owner?
4. What had the passenger done before Carlos arrived?

## 📝 Writing

Write a short news story (120–150 words) about a real or invented event. Use the past simple for the main events and the past perfect for background.

**Model:**

> DOG FINDS ITS WAY HOME AFTER 300 KM  
>   
> A family in Goiânia had a big surprise last week when their dog, Thor, appeared at their door. Thor had disappeared six months earlier during a family trip to Brasília. The family had searched for him for weeks and had posted his photo on social media, but they had almost given up. "We had already accepted that we would never see him again," said Marina, 34. Experts believe Thor had walked more than 300 kilometers. When he arrived, he was thin and tired, but healthy. A veterinarian confirmed it was Thor because he had been microchipped as a puppy. The family says they had never been so happy.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Linguagem de notícias

Em notícias em inglês, você vai ver muito **"It turned out that..."**, **"According to..."** (segundo...), **"Police said..."** e **"It is believed that..."** (acredita-se que). Ler uma notícia curta por dia em sites como BBC News ou AP é um ótimo treino de B1.
