## 🧩 Grammar: Subject x object questions

Quando a pergunta é sobre o **objeto** (quem **recebe** a ação), use o auxiliar normal (do/does/did):
- "**Who did** you **call**?" → "I called **my mom**." (você ligou para alguém — quem?)
- "**What did** she **buy**?" → "She bought **a car**."

Quando a pergunta é sobre o **sujeito** (quem **faz** a ação), **não use auxiliar** — a question word **é** o sujeito e o verbo fica na forma afirmativa:
- "**Who called** you?" → "**My mom** called me." (alguém ligou — quem?)
- "**What happened**?" (❌ "What did happen?")
- "**Which** app **works** best?"
- "**How many people came** to the party?"

Compare:
- **Who loves** Ana? (alguém ama a Ana — quem?) → Pedro loves Ana.
- **Who does** Ana **love**? (a Ana ama alguém — quem?) → Ana loves Pedro.

**Perguntas indiretas** (mais educadas): depois de "Do you know...?" / "Can you tell me...?", use a ordem de **afirmativa**:
- "Where **is** the bank?" → "Do you know where **the bank is**?"
- "Who **did** you **call**?" → "Can you tell me who **you called**?"

| Pergunta de sujeito (sem auxiliar) | Pergunta de objeto (com auxiliar) |
|---|---|
| Who called you? | Who did you call? |
| Who recommended this place? | Who did you recommend? |
| What made you laugh? | What did you make? |
| Which team won? | Which team did you support? |
| How many people live here? | How many people do you know here? |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M4-L4/example-01.m4a) Who fixed your laptop? — A guy my brother recommended. — _Quem consertou seu notebook? — Um cara que meu irmão indicou._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M4-L4/example-02.m4a) Who did you hire to paint the house? — _Quem você contratou para pintar a casa?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M4-L4/example-03.m4a) Do you know who designed this logo? — _Você sabe quem fez esse logo?_

> 💡 **Dica:** **What happened?** e **Who knows?** são perguntas de sujeito superfrequentes — nunca use "did" nelas.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ What did happen?  
  ✅ **What happened?**  
  What é o sujeito: sem auxiliar.
- ❌ Who did call you?  
  ✅ **Who called you?**  
  Who é o sujeito (quem ligou): sem auxiliar.
- ❌ Do you know where is the station?  
  ✅ **Do you know where the station is?**  
  Perguntas indiretas usam ordem de afirmativa.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Entonação em perguntas indiretas

Perguntas indiretas que começam com **Do you know / Can you tell me** têm entonação de pergunta de sim/não — sobem no final: "Do you know where the station is? ↗". Isso as deixa mais suaves e educadas.

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M4-L4/pron-01.m4a) Who called you? ↘

## 📖 Reading — The power of word of mouth [🔊](../../../audio/B1/B1-M4-L4/reading.m4a)

Who do you trust more: an advertisement or a friend? For most people, the answer is obvious. Marketing studies have repeatedly shown that personal recommendations are among the most trusted forms of advertising. That's why so many businesses invest in referral programs: "Recommend a friend and you both get 20% off." Online reviews have become a digital version of word of mouth — but they have a problem. Who writes them? Some companies pay for fake five-star reviews, and some competitors post fake negative ones. How can you tell what's real? Experts suggest reading the three-star reviews, which are often the most honest, looking for specific details, and checking whether the same reviewer has written many similar reviews. And, of course, asking someone you actually know.

**Questions:**
1. What have marketing studies shown?
2. How do referral programs work?
3. What's the problem with online reviews?
4. Why should you read three-star reviews?

## 📝 Writing

Write a message (80–100 words) recommending a professional or business you trust to a friend. Say who it is, who recommended them to you and why you can vouch for them.

**Model:**

> Hi Carol! You asked who did my kitchen, right? It was a guy called Seu Jorge — he's a carpenter and he comes highly recommended. My neighbor recommended him after he built her wardrobe, and I've had three things done by him since then. He's reliable, he always gives you a fair quote before starting, and he finishes on time (rare!). He isn't the cheapest, but he's definitely not a rip-off. I can vouch for him 100%. I'll send you his number — tell him I sent you! 😊

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Referências no mercado de trabalho

Em países de língua inglesa, **references** (referências) e **referrals** (indicações) têm grande peso em contratações. É comum o empregador ligar para um ex-chefe seu. E muitas vagas são preenchidas por indicação interna — por isso o **networking** é tão valorizado.
