## 🧩 Grammar: Nominalisation and formal register

**Nominalização** = transformar **verbos** ou **adjetivos** em **substantivos**. O resultado é um texto mais **denso, impessoal e objetivo** — o estilo de relatórios, artigos e textos acadêmicos.

**Comparação:**
- Fala: "Prices **went up** sharply, so people **bought** less."
- Formal: "The sharp **rise** in prices led to a **fall** in consumption."

**Sufixos comuns:**
- **-tion / -sion:** introduce → introduction, decide → decision, expand → expansion
- **-ment:** develop → development, improve → improvement
- **-ance / -ence:** perform → performance, depend → dependence
- **-ity:** flexible → flexibility, secure → security
- **-ness:** aware → awareness, effective → effectiveness
- **-al:** arrive → arrival, refuse → refusal
- Mesma forma: increase, decline, rise, fall, change, cut, demand

**Como nominalizar:**
1. Transforme o verbo principal em substantivo.
2. O sujeito vira **possessivo** ou **of**: "The government **regulated** apps" → "The government's **regulation** of apps" / "**the regulation of** apps **by** the government".
3. Advérbio vira **adjetivo**: "increased **sharply**" → "a **sharp** increase".
4. Conectores viram **verbos/preposições de causa**: because/so → **led to, resulted in, caused, due to, as a result of**.

**Collocations formais com substantivos:**
carry out (research / an investigation / a survey) · make (an improvement / a recommendation / a contribution) · have (an impact / an effect on) · take (measures / steps / action) · reach (a conclusion / an agreement) · pose (a threat / a risk)

⚠️ **Cuidado com o exagero:** nominalização demais deixa o texto pesado e difícil. Equilibre com verbos fortes.

| Estilo verbal (fala) | Estilo nominal (formal) |
|---|---|
| Many people lost their jobs. | There were widespread job losses. |
| The company decided to close the plant. | The company's decision to close the plant... |
| Workers are not secure. | There is a lack of job security. |
| Sales fell dramatically. | There was a dramatic fall in sales. |
| They introduced apps, so work became more flexible. | The introduction of apps resulted in greater flexibility. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/C1/C1-M2-L2/example-01.m4a) The rapid growth of delivery apps has resulted in a sharp increase in the number of platform workers. — _O rápido crescimento dos apps de entrega resultou num aumento acentuado do número de trabalhadores por plataforma._
- [🔊](../../../audio/C1/C1-M2-L2/example-02.m4a) The lack of social protection for these workers poses a serious challenge for policymakers. — _A falta de proteção social para esses trabalhadores representa um sério desafio para os formuladores de políticas._
- [🔊](../../../audio/C1/C1-M2-L2/example-03.m4a) The implementation of a four-day week led to a noticeable improvement in staff well-being. — _A implementação de uma semana de quatro dias levou a uma melhora perceptível no bem-estar da equipe._

> 💡 **Dica:** Português formal também nominaliza muito ("a implementação de", "o aumento de"). O desafio em inglês é escolher a **collocation** certa: **carry out** research (não "make research"), **take** measures, **reach** a conclusion.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ There was a dramatically increase in prices.  
  ✅ **There was a dramatic increase in prices.**  
  Antes de substantivo, use adjetivo (dramatic), não advérbio.
- ❌ They made a research about the gig economy.  
  ✅ **They carried out research into the gig economy.**  
  carry out research (incontável) + into/on.
- ❌ The introduce of apps changed work.  
  ✅ **The introduction of apps changed work.**  
  Use a forma de substantivo (introduction).

**Journalist:** One of the most talked-about experiments in the world of work in recent years has been the four-day week. In 2022, a large pilot programme in the UK involved around sixty companies, whose employees moved to a four-day week with no loss of pay, on the condition that productivity was maintained. The results, published in 2023, attracted international attention. Most of the participating companies said they would continue with the new schedule. Researchers reported reductions in stress and burnout among employees, as well as improvements in sleep and work-life balance. Similar trials have since been carried out in other countries, including Brazil. Critics point out, however, that the companies involved chose to take part, which may have influenced the results, and that the model is far harder to implement in sectors such as healthcare or retail. ## 📖 Reading — Working for the algorithm [🔊](../../../audio/C1/C1-M2-L2/reading.m4a)

The expansion of app-based work has been one of the most significant changes in the labour market of the past decade. In Brazil, as in many other countries, the growth of ride-hailing and food delivery platforms has created opportunities for hundreds of thousands of people, particularly during periods of high unemployment. For many workers, the attraction lies in the flexibility these platforms offer: the ability to choose when and how long to work, and the absence of a boss in the traditional sense. However, this flexibility comes at a price. Unlike conventional employees, most platform workers do not have access to paid holidays, sick pay or guaranteed minimum earnings. Their income depends on fluctuations in demand and on decisions made by algorithms over which they have little control or understanding. The suspension of an account, for example, can mean the immediate loss of a worker's livelihood, often without a clear explanation. These concerns have led to growing pressure for regulation. Governments in several countries, including Brazil, have attempted to introduce rules guaranteeing minimum standards of pay and social protection, though such proposals have frequently met with resistance from both companies and some workers, who fear the loss of flexibility. The challenge for policymakers, therefore, lies in the development of a model that combines the advantages of flexibility with an adequate level of security. The outcome of this debate will have a profound impact not only on platform workers, but on the future of work as a whole.

## 📝 Writing

Write a formal report (280–320 words) for your company's HR director on the results of a six-month hybrid working trial. Use headings (Introduction, Findings, Recommendations, Conclusion) and nominalisation throughout.

**Model:**

> Report on the hybrid working trial  
>   
> Introduction  
> The aim of this report is to evaluate the results of the six-month hybrid working trial carried out in the marketing and finance departments between January and June, and to make recommendations regarding its continuation. The findings are based on an online survey of 85 employees, interviews with team leaders and an analysis of performance data.  
>   
> Findings  
> Overall, the introduction of hybrid working was well received. Approximately 80% of respondents reported an improvement in their work-life balance, largely as a result of the reduction in commuting time. Performance data showed no decline in productivity; in fact, the finance department recorded a slight increase in the number of tasks completed on time.  
>   
> However, the trial also revealed some concerns. Several employees mentioned a lack of contact with colleagues and a sense of isolation, particularly among new staff. Team leaders also highlighted difficulties in the coordination of projects when team members were rarely in the office on the same days. In addition, the absence of clear guidelines led to some confusion regarding availability and response times.  
>   
> Recommendations  
> In light of these findings, the following measures are recommended:  
> • the continuation of the hybrid model, with a minimum of two office days per week;  
> • the introduction of fixed "team days" to facilitate collaboration;  
> • the creation of a mentoring programme for new employees;  
> • the publication of clear guidelines on working hours and communication.  
>   
> Conclusion  
> The trial has demonstrated that hybrid working can lead to greater employee satisfaction without any loss of productivity. Provided that the issues of isolation and coordination are addressed, the adoption of the model on a permanent basis would be of considerable benefit to the company.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "Plain English"

Em países anglófonos, há um movimento forte por **Plain English**: governos e empresas são incentivados a escrever de forma clara e direta, evitando jargão e nominalização excessiva. No Reino Unido, a **Plain English Campaign** existe desde 1979. A lição: use estilo formal quando o gênero exige — mas clareza vem sempre primeiro.
