## 🧩 Grammar: Present perfect x past simple (nível B1+)

Você já conhece a regra básica (tempo terminado x aberto). No B1+, veja como os dois tempos **trabalham juntos** em textos de não ficção e notícias:

**1. Present perfect para apresentar o tema ou resultado atual → past simple para os detalhes:**
"Scientists **have studied** self-control for decades. The first famous experiment **took place** at Stanford in the 1960s."

**2. Present perfect com `since` + past simple na outra parte:**
"**Since** the study **was published**, many researchers **have tried** to replicate it."

**3. Superlativo + present perfect (ever):**
"It's **the most famous** psychology experiment I**'ve ever read** about."

**4. `It's the first time` + present perfect:**
"**It's the first time** I**'ve saved** money for a whole year."

**5. Expressões de período aberto (present perfect):** recently, lately, so far, up to now, over the past few years, in the last decade
"**Over the past few years**, researchers **have questioned** the original conclusions."

**Expressões de tempo fechado (past simple):** in the 1960s, at that time, back then, when I was..., years ago, at first
"**At that time**, nobody **questioned** the results."

| Present perfect (até agora) | Past simple (fechado) |
|---|---|
| Scientists have studied it for 50 years. | The first study took place in 1968. |
| Over the past decade, many people have questioned it. | In 2018, a new study was published. |
| I've never resisted chocolate. | Yesterday I ate a whole box. |
| Since she started saving, she has bought a car. | She started saving in 2022. |
| It's the first time I've done this. | The first time I did it, I was 15. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M2-L2/example-01.m4a) Over the last few years, researchers have found that family background also matters. — _Nos últimos anos, pesquisadores descobriram que a origem familiar também importa._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M2-L2/example-02.m4a) When the children took the test, nobody knew it would become so famous. — _Quando as crianças fizeram o teste, ninguém sabia que ficaria tão famoso._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M2-L2/example-03.m4a) It's the first time I've managed to stay off social media for a week. — _É a primeira vez que consigo ficar uma semana fora das redes sociais._

> 💡 **Dica:** Em resumos de estudos e palestras, um padrão muito útil é: **"Researchers have found that... In one study, they... The results showed that..."** (present perfect → past simple → past simple).

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Since the study was published, many scientists tried to replicate it.  
  ✅ **Since the study was published, many scientists have tried to replicate it.**  
  A parte principal com since (até agora) = present perfect.
- ❌ It's the first time I do this.  
  ✅ **It's the first time I've done this.**  
  It's the first time + present perfect.
- ❌ The experiment has taken place in 1968.  
  ✅ **The experiment took place in 1968.**  
  Data definida = past simple.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Formas fracas: have / has / been

Em textos falados (palestras, podcasts), **have** e **has** quase desaparecem: "Researchers've found" = "risârtcherz-v fáund". Treine o ouvido: o particípio (found, studied, shown) é a pista principal.

## 📖 Reading — What the marshmallow test really tells us [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M2-L2/reading.m4a)

The marshmallow test is one of the most famous experiments in psychology. In the late 1960s and early 1970s, researchers at Stanford University offered preschool children a choice: eat one treat now, or wait alone for about fifteen minutes and get two. Years later, follow-up studies suggested that the children who had waited tended to have better school results and other positive outcomes. The idea spread quickly: self-control, it seemed, was a key to success. Since then, however, the story has become more complicated. The original groups were small and mostly came from families connected to the university. In 2018, a larger study with a more diverse group of children found a much weaker connection, and much of it seemed to be explained by factors like family income and education. Other researchers have shown that children wait longer when they trust the adult who made the promise. So the test may measure not only willpower, but also experience: if promises in your life have often been broken, eating the marshmallow now is a rational choice.

**Questions:**
1. What choice did the children have?
2. What did follow-up studies suggest?
3. What was the problem with the original groups?
4. Why might eating the marshmallow now be a rational choice?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Replication crisis

O teste do marshmallow faz parte de um debate maior: a **replication crisis** (crise de replicação), quando estudos famosos não se confirmam ao serem repetidos. Na hora de citar ciência em inglês, use linguagem cuidadosa: **"Research suggests..."**, **"Some studies indicate..."** em vez de **"Science proves..."**.

## ✅ Resumo
- Present perfect apresenta o tema → past simple dá os detalhes.
- Since + past simple, present perfect.
- It's the first time + present perfect.
- Linguagem cuidadosa: research suggests / studies indicate.
