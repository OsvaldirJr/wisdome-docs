# Review — Work and Trends
**B1P-M1** — Revise present simple x continuous avançado, verb patterns, used to/would e perguntas indiretas.

## O que você aprendeu neste módulo
- Discutir o que torna um trabalho significativo
- Diferenciar ações de estados (present simple x continuous, stative verbs)
- Usar verb patterns com segurança para falar de habilidades e qualidades
- Contar como uma tendência ou movimento começou
- Responder perguntas de entrevista com perguntas indiretas e estrutura clara

## ✏️ Review exercises

### Exercise 1 — Choose the correct option

_Choose the correct option._

1. I ___ about quitting my job.  
   ( ) think · ( ) 'm thinking
2. My boss wants ___ the report today.  
   ( ) that I finish · ( ) me to finish · ( ) me finish
3. My parents never ___ me stay out late.  
   ( ) let · ( ) allowed · ( ) made to
4. I ___ live in a small town. Now I live in São Paulo.  
   ( ) use to · ( ) used to · ( ) am used to
5. Could you tell me where ___?  
   ( ) is the office · ( ) the office is

### Exercise 2 — Mixed practice

_Complete with the correct form._

1. I'm not used to ___ (work) at night.
2. She's capable ___ leading the team.
3. My teacher encouraged me ___ (apply) for the scholarship.
4. Do you know if the company ___ (offer) training?

## 🎤 Free conversation

Grave um vídeo de 3 minutos como se fosse um "video pitch" para uma vaga internacional: quem você é, como sua carreira mudou (used to / now), o que você tem de melhor (verb patterns) e duas perguntas indiretas que faria à empresa.

1. What makes a job meaningful for you?
2. What did you use to want to be when you were a child?
3. Who encouraged you in your career or studies?
4. What trend in your industry do you think will last?
5. What would you like to know about a company before accepting an offer?
