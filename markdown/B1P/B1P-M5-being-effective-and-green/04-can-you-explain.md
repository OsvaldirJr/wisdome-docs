## 🧩 Grammar: Linguagem de esclarecimento

Esclarecer envolve três movimentos — e usa estruturas que você já domina (perguntas indiretas, modais, reformulação):

**1. PEDIR esclarecimento:**
- Não ouviu: "Sorry, I **didn't (quite) catch** that." / "Could you **say that again**, please?"
- Não entendeu uma palavra: "What **exactly do you mean by** 'scalable'?" / "What does '...' **mean**?"
- Quer mais detalhes: "Could you **elaborate** on that?" / "Could you **give me an example**?"
- Perguntas indiretas (mais educadas): "**I'm not sure I understand what** you mean." / "**Could you explain how** that works?"

**2. CHECAR o que você entendeu (parafrasear):**
- "**So, if I understand correctly**, you want us to...?"
- "**So what you're saying is**...?" / "**Do you mean that**...?"
- "**In other words**, ...?"

**3. DAR esclarecimento / reformular:**
- "**Let me put it another way.**" / "**What I mean is**..." / "**In plain English**, ..."
- "**To give you an example**, ..." / "**Basically**, ..." / "**The bottom line is**..."
- Checar se o outro entendeu (sem parecer arrogante): "**Does that make sense?**" / "**Is that clear?**" (cuidado: "Do you understand?" pode soar condescendente)

**Corrigir um mal-entendido com educação:**
"**Sorry, I think there's been a misunderstanding.** What I meant was..." / "**That's not quite what I meant.**"

| Função | Frases |
|---|---|
| Não ouvi | Sorry, I didn't catch that. / Could you repeat that? |
| Não entendi | What exactly do you mean by...? / Could you explain...? |
| Mais detalhes | Could you elaborate? / Could you give me an example? |
| Parafrasear | So, if I understand correctly... / So what you're saying is... |
| Reformular | Let me put it another way. / What I mean is... |
| Checar | Does that make sense? / Am I making myself clear? |
| Corrigir | That's not quite what I meant. / There's been a misunderstanding. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L4/example-01.m4a) Sorry, what exactly do you mean by "carbon neutral"? Could you give me an example? — _Desculpe, o que exatamente você quer dizer com "carbono neutro"? Você poderia me dar um exemplo?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L4/example-02.m4a) So, if I understand correctly, the deadline has been moved to next Friday? — _Então, se eu entendi bem, o prazo foi adiado para a próxima sexta?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L4/example-03.m4a) Let me put it another way: we don't need more money, we need more time. — _Deixa eu dizer de outra forma: não precisamos de mais dinheiro, precisamos de mais tempo._

> 💡 **Dica:** Em reuniões internacionais, **até nativos** pedem esclarecimento o tempo todo — é sinal de atenção, não de fraqueza. Fingir entender e errar depois é muito pior do que perguntar.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ What do you want to say with 'scalable'?  
  ✅ **What do you mean by 'scalable'?**  
  "Querer dizer" = mean (não "want to say" — tradução literal).
- ❌ Do you understand me? (para um colega)  
  ✅ **Does that make sense? / Is that clear?**  
  "Do you understand?" pode soar condescendente.
- ❌ I didn't understand nothing.  
  ✅ **I didn't understand anything. / I didn't quite follow.**  
  Inglês não aceita negação dupla.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Ênfase na palavra-chave

Ao pedir esclarecimento sobre uma palavra, dê **ênfase** nela: "What do you mean by **SCALABLE**?" Ao corrigir um mal-entendido, enfatize a informação certa: "I said **FRIDAY**, not Monday."

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L4/pron-01.m4a) What do you mean by SCALABLE?

## 📖 Reading — Lost in translation [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L4/reading.m4a)

Misunderstandings between cultures are rarely about grammar. More often, they come from different expectations about how directly people should speak. Researchers on intercultural communication describe some cultures — like the Netherlands, Germany or Israel — as "low-context": people tend to say exactly what they mean, and the words carry most of the message. Other cultures — like Japan, China or much of Latin America, including Brazil — are often described as "high-context": a lot of the meaning comes from tone, relationships and what is not said. This can cause confusion. When a British colleague says "That's an interesting idea", they may actually mean "I don't agree". When a Brazilian says "Let's meet up sometime!", it may be a friendly expression rather than a real plan. Experts suggest a simple strategy for international teams: when in doubt, check. Paraphrase what you understood ("So you'd like me to...?"), ask for concrete details (dates, numbers, next steps) and never assume that silence means agreement.

**Questions:**
1. Where do cultural misunderstandings usually come from?
2. What is a "low-context" culture?
3. What might a British colleague mean by "That's an interesting idea"?
4. What strategy do experts suggest?

## 🎤 Speaking

"Jargon challenge": each person explains something using as much jargon from their field as possible. The listeners must interrupt politely to ask for clarification, paraphrase and check until everything is clear.

1. Have you ever had a misunderstanding because of language?
2. Are Brazilians high-context or low-context communicators? Give examples.
3. How do you check that people understood your instructions?
4. What's a word in your field that outsiders never understand?
5. Is it hard for you to interrupt someone to ask a question?

## 📝 Writing

Write an email (120–150 words) after a confusing meeting: summarize what you understood, ask for clarification on two specific points and confirm the next steps.

**Model:**

> Subject: Follow-up – sustainability meeting  
>   
> Hi Robert,  
>   
> Thank you for today's meeting. I'd just like to check that I understood everything correctly.  
>   
> As I understand it, all our offices need to become carbon-neutral by September, and each team has to present a plan to reduce energy use by the end of June. Is that right?  
>   
> I also have two questions. First, when you mentioned "compensating unavoidable emissions", did you mean that each team is responsible for this, or will it be managed centrally? Second, could you clarify what budget is available for changes like new lighting or equipment?  
>   
> If my understanding is correct, our next step is to collect energy data for the last twelve months and share it with you by May 15th. Please let me know if I've misunderstood anything.  
>   
> Best regards,  
> Luana

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "British English translated"

Uma piada famosa na internet "traduz" o inglês britânico educado: **"With the greatest respect..."** = acho que você está errado; **"I'll bear it in mind"** = já esqueci; **"Quite good"** = um pouco decepcionante; **"Very interesting"** = isso não faz sentido. Exagero, claro — mas mostra por que checar é importante!
