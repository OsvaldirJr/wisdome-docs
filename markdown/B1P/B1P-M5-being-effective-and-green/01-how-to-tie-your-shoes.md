## 🧩 Grammar: Instruções: imperativos + condicionais + time clauses

Instruções claras em inglês combinam três estruturas:

**1. Imperativos** (verbo base, sem sujeito):
- "**Hold** the lace in your right hand." / "**Don't pull** too hard." / "**Be careful not to** cut yourself."
- Com **sequenciadores**: First, ... / Then, ... / Next, ... / After that, ... / Finally, ...

**2. Zero conditional** → resultados sempre verdadeiros (como funciona):
- "If you **pull** both ends, the knot **gets** tighter."
- "When the water **boils**, the light **turns** off."

**3. First conditional** → consequências e avisos:
- "If you **tie** it this way, it **won't** come undone."
- "If you **don't** save the file, you**'ll lose** everything."

**Imperativo + if** (dicas): "**If** the app freezes, **restart** your phone." / "**If** in doubt, **ask**."

**Time clauses** (presente simples, mesmo falando do futuro):
- "**Once** you**'ve tied** the first knot, **make** a loop." (once + present perfect = depois que você tiver feito)
- "**As soon as** the light **turns** green, **press** start."
- "**Unless** the instructions **say** otherwise, **use** cold water."

| Função | Estrutura | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Instrução | imperativo | Fold the paper in half. |
| Proibição/aviso | Don't + verbo / Be careful not to | Don't touch the screen. |
| Como funciona | If + present, present | If you press here, it opens. |
| Consequência | If + present, will | If you skip this step, it won't work. |
| Sequência | Once / As soon as + present (perfect) | Once you've finished, click Save. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L1/example-01.m4a) First, cross the laces. Then, make a loop with the right lace. Be careful not to pull too hard. — _Primeiro, cruze os cadarços. Depois, faça um laço com o cadarço direito. Cuidado para não puxar demais._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L1/example-02.m4a) If you tie it the other way around, the knot will stay in place all day. — _Se você amarrar ao contrário, o nó vai ficar firme o dia todo._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L1/example-03.m4a) Once you've uploaded the file, you'll receive a confirmation email. — _Depois que você enviar o arquivo, vai receber um e-mail de confirmação._

> 💡 **Dica:** Em instruções escritas (manuais, receitas, tutoriais), o inglês é **direto e curto**. Não use "you should" para cada passo — o imperativo é educado nesse contexto.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ You must to press the button.  
  ✅ **Press the button.**  
  Em instruções, o imperativo é natural e claro.
- ❌ If you will press it, it opens.  
  ✅ **If you press it, it opens.**  
  Depois de if, presente simples.
- ❌ Don't to touch the screen.  
  ✅ **Don't touch the screen.**  
  Imperativo negativo: don't + verbo base.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Entonação em listas e instruções

Em sequências de passos, a voz **sobe** no fim de cada passo (indicando que vem mais) e **desce** no último: "First, cross the laces ↗. Then, make a loop ↗. Finally, pull ↘."

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L1/pron-01.m4a) First, cross the laces ↗.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L1/pron-02.m4a) Then, make a loop ↗.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L1/pron-03.m4a) Finally, pull both ends ↘. And that's it!

## 📖 Reading — Small changes, big results [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L1/reading.m4a)

Most of us want to be more productive, but we often look for big solutions: a new job, a new app, a complete change of routine. Productivity experts suggest the opposite. If you make small, specific changes, they'll add up over time. Here are some research-backed ideas. If you check your email first thing in the morning, you'll spend the day reacting to other people's priorities — so start with your most important task instead. When you work on a difficult task, turn off notifications: some studies suggest that it can take a long time to fully refocus after an interruption. Use the "two-minute rule": if something takes less than two minutes, do it immediately. And unless you have a very good reason, don't multitask. Our brains don't really do two things at once; they switch quickly between them, and every switch costs energy. As the saying goes, "If you chase two rabbits, you'll catch neither."

**Questions:**
1. What do experts suggest instead of big solutions?
2. What happens if you check email first thing in the morning?

4. Why shouldn't you multitask?

## 📝 Writing

Write a step-by-step tutorial (150–180 words) for something you know how to do well. Use imperatives, sequencers and at least 3 conditionals or time clauses.

**Model:**

> How to make the perfect Brazilian rice  
>   
> First of all, rinse one cup of rice in cold water until the water runs clear. If you skip this step, the rice will be sticky.  
>   
> Then, heat two tablespoons of oil in a pan and add half a chopped onion and one clove of garlic. Be careful not to burn the garlic — if it turns dark, it'll taste bitter.  
>   
> Once the onion is soft, add the rice and stir for about two minutes. This makes the grains loose and fluffy.  
>   
> Next, add two cups of hot water and a teaspoon of salt. As soon as the water boils, turn the heat down to low and cover the pan.  
>   
> Cook for about 15 minutes. Don't open the lid and don't stir! When the water has disappeared and you see little holes on the surface, turn off the heat.  
>   
> Finally, leave the rice covered for five more minutes. Make sure you fluff it with a fork before serving. And that's it — perfect rice, every time!

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — IKEA e o inglês das instruções

As instruções da IKEA são famosas por não terem quase nenhuma palavra — só desenhos! Mas manuais, receitas e tutoriais em inglês seguem um padrão: verbos no imperativo no começo de cada passo (**Insert**, **Turn**, **Press**, **Add**). Ler manuais em inglês é um ótimo treino de vocabulário prático.

## ✅ Resumo
- Instruções: imperativo + sequenciadores (First, Then, Finally).
- If + present, present (como funciona) / will (consequência).
- Once / as soon as / unless + presente.
- Make sure you... / Be careful not to... / And that's it!
