## 🧩 Grammar: Passive voice: all tenses

No B1 você aprendeu a passiva no presente e no passado simples. A regra é sempre a mesma: **be (no tempo certo) + particípio**. Só muda o **be**:

- **Present simple:** am/is/are + particípio → "Plastic **is recycled** here."
- **Present continuous:** am/is/are **being** + particípio → "A new park **is being built**." (está sendo construído)
- **Past simple:** was/were + particípio → "The garden **was created** in 2015."
- **Past continuous:** was/were **being** + particípio → "The river **was being cleaned** when I visited."
- **Present perfect:** has/have **been** + particípio → "More than 1,000 trees **have been planted**."
- **Future (will):** will **be** + particípio → "The results **will be published** next year."
- **Going to:** am/is/are going to **be** + particípio → "The old factory **is going to be turned** into a park."
- **Modais:** can / should / must / might **be** + particípio → "Glass **can be recycled** forever." / "Food waste **should be composted**."

**Para descrever processos**, a passiva é a forma mais natural, com sequenciadores:
"First, the bottles **are collected**. Then they**'re washed** and **cut** into small pieces. After that, the pieces **are melted** and **turned** into new products."

**Get + particípio** (informal) às vezes substitui be: "My bike **got stolen**." / "The project **got cancelled**."

| Tempo | Passiva | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | is/are + pp | Paper is recycled every week. |
| Present continuous | is/are being + pp | The beach is being cleaned. |
| Past simple | was/were + pp | The park was opened in 2020. |
| Present perfect | has/have been + pp | Ten tons of trash have been removed. |
| Future | will be + pp | New bins will be installed. |
| Modal | can/should/must be + pp | Batteries must be disposed of safely. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L3/example-01.m4a) In the last five years, more than 200 community gardens have been created in São Paulo. — _Nos últimos cinco anos, mais de 200 hortas comunitárias foram criadas em São Paulo._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L3/example-02.m4a) The old landfill is being transformed into a solar farm. — _O antigo aterro está sendo transformado numa usina solar._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L3/example-03.m4a) Most food waste could be avoided if meals were planned better. — _A maior parte do desperdício de alimentos poderia ser evitada se as refeições fossem mais bem planejadas._

> 💡 **Dica:** Em relatórios e textos formais, a passiva ajuda a manter o foco no **resultado**, não em quem fez: "Mistakes **were made**" (cuidado: às vezes é usada para **esconder** responsabilidade!).

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ The bridge is building now.  
  ✅ **The bridge is being built now.**  
  Passiva no presente contínuo = is being + particípio.
- ❌ Many trees have planted this year.  
  ✅ **Many trees have been planted this year.**  
  Present perfect passivo = have been + particípio.
- ❌ This can recycle.  
  ✅ **This can be recycled.**  
  Modal + be + particípio.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Being e been

**Being** = "BÍ-in" (duas sílabas). **Been** = "bin" (EUA) / "bín" (UK) — uma sílaba, fraca. Confundir os dois muda o tempo verbal: "is **being** built" (agora) x "has **been** built" (concluído).

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L3/pron-01.m4a) It's being built. ("BÍ-in")
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L3/pron-02.m4a) It's been built. ("bin")
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L3/pron-03.m4a) Trees have been planted.

<details>
<summary>Transcript</summary>

**Guide:** Welcome to our cooperative. Let me show you how plastic bottles are recycled. First, the bottles are collected from homes and businesses by our members. Then they're sorted by color and type — this step is still done by hand. After that, the labels and caps are removed, and the bottles are washed. Next, they're cut into small flakes, which are melted and turned into pellets. Finally, these pellets are sold to factories, where they're used to make new products — clothes, carpets, even new bottles. Last year, more than 500 tons of plastic were processed here, and our cooperative has been recognized as one of the most efficient in the state.

## 📖 Reading — Brazil's recyclers [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M5-L3/reading.m4a)

In Brazil, a large part of the waste that gets recycled is collected not by machines or big companies, but by people: the catadores, or waste pickers. Estimates suggest that hundreds of thousands of Brazilians work in this activity, many of them in cooperatives. For decades, their work was largely invisible and badly paid. In recent years, however, their role has been increasingly recognized. A national policy on solid waste, approved in 2010, encouraged cities to include waste picker cooperatives in their recycling systems, and in many places their work is now being formally contracted by local governments. Aluminum cans are a famous success story: Brazil has recycled a very high proportion of its aluminum cans for many years, largely thanks to the work of catadores. Challenges remain — many waste pickers still work in difficult and unsafe conditions — but their story shows that environmental solutions can't be separated from social justice.

## 📝 Writing

Write a short report (150–180 words) about an environmental project (real or invented): what problem existed, what has been done, what is being done now and what will be done next. Use at least 6 passive forms.

**Model:**

> Project report: Green Schools Initiative  
>   
> Three years ago, our city's public schools produced more than 20 tons of food waste every month, and almost nothing was recycled. In 2023, the Green Schools Initiative was launched by a group of teachers and parents.  
>   
> Since then, a lot has been achieved. Compost bins have been installed in 45 schools, and more than 300 tons of organic waste have been turned into fertilizer. This fertilizer is used in school gardens, where vegetables are grown by the students themselves and served in the cafeteria.  
>   
> Currently, a new recycling system is being tested in ten schools: plastic and paper are separated in each classroom and collected by a local waste pickers' cooperative.  
>   
> Next year, the project will be expanded to all 120 schools in the city, and a training program for cafeteria staff is going to be created. The initiative shows that big problems can be solved through small, everyday actions — especially when the whole community gets involved.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Reduce, reuse, recycle

Os famosos **3 Rs** em ordem de importância: **Reduce** (reduzir — o mais eficaz), **Reuse** (reutilizar) e **Recycle** (reciclar — o último recurso). Hoje muitos ativistas acrescentam **Refuse** (recusar o que você não precisa) e **Rot** (compostar). Outro termo comum: **greenwashing** — quando empresas fingem ser mais "verdes" do que são.
