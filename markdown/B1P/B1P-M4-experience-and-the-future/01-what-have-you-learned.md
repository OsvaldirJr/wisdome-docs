## 🧩 Grammar: Superlativos + present perfect · the first time

**Superlativo + present perfect + ever** → a maior/melhor/pior experiência **até agora**:
- "It's **the most inspiring** talk I**'ve ever seen**."
- "She's **the kindest** person I**'ve ever met**."
- "That's **the hardest** thing I**'ve ever done**."
(o **that/who** antes do sujeito pode ser omitido: "the best book (that) I've ever read")

**It's the first/second/only time + present perfect** → experiência vivida agora:
- "**It's the first time** I**'ve spoken** in public."
- "**This is the third time** I**'ve visited** Lisbon."

⚠️ No **passado**, a estrutura muda para **past perfect**: "**It was the first time** I**'d ever seen** snow." (No A2/B1 você viu o past perfect — aqui ele volta!)

**Never... before** → também expressa primeira vez: "I**'ve never** worked with such a diverse team **before**."

**Expressões de aprendizado:**
- "I**'ve learned** (how) **to** + verbo": "I've learned to listen more."
- "I**'ve learned that** + frase": "I've learned that everyone communicates differently."
- "X **has taught me to/that**...": "My brother has taught me to be patient."

| Estrutura | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| the + superlativo + I've ever + particípio | It's the best decision I've ever made. |
| It's the first time + present perfect | It's the first time I've tried this. |
| It was the first time + past perfect | It was the first time I'd flown alone. |
| never... before | I've never felt so welcome before. |
| has taught me to / that | Life has taught me that nothing is permanent. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L1/example-01.m4a) My sister, who is autistic, has taught me to see details that most people miss. — _Minha irmã, que é autista, me ensinou a ver detalhes que a maioria das pessoas não percebe._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L1/example-02.m4a) Volunteering abroad was the most eye-opening experience I've ever had. — _Ser voluntário no exterior foi a experiência mais reveladora que já tive._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L1/example-03.m4a) It was the first time I'd ever been in a country where I didn't speak the language. — _Foi a primeira vez que eu estava num país onde não falava a língua._

> 💡 **Dica:** Ao falar de deficiência, prefira linguagem que a própria comunidade usa. Muitos preferem **"person with a disability"** (person-first) e muitos autistas preferem **"autistic person"** (identity-first). Na dúvida, pergunte ou siga como a pessoa se descreve.

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ It's the best movie I ever saw.  
  ✅ **It's the best movie I've ever seen.**  
  Superlativo + experiência até agora = present perfect (padrão britânico e de provas).
- ❌ It's the first time I come here.  
  ✅ **It's the first time I've come here.**  
  It's the first time + present perfect.
- ❌ It was the first time I've seen snow.  
  ✅ **It was the first time I'd seen snow.**  
  No passado (It was), use past perfect.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Ever / I've ever

**I've ever** se junta: "**áivéver**". Na frase, a força vai no superlativo e no particípio: "the **BEST** book I've ever **READ**". **Eye-opening** = "ÁI-ôupenin".

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L1/pron-01.m4a) It's the BEST book I've ever READ.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L1/pron-02.m4a) It's the first time I've DONE this.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L1/pron-03.m4a) It was a real EYE-opener.

## 📖 Reading — Different minds at work [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L1/reading.m4a)

For a long time, many workplaces were designed for one type of brain. Job interviews rewarded people who were good at small talk and eye contact, open-plan offices were full of noise, and instructions were often vague. As a result, many talented neurodivergent people — including autistic people and people with ADHD or dyslexia — struggled to find or keep jobs. In recent years, some large companies have started neurodiversity hiring programs. Instead of traditional interviews, candidates might spend a few days solving real problems, and managers receive training on how to give clear, direct feedback. Several of these companies report that neurodivergent employees bring valuable strengths, such as pattern recognition, attention to detail, creative problem-solving and deep focus. Advocates point out, however, that the goal shouldn't only be productivity. "Inclusion isn't about finding hidden superpowers," one autistic activist explains. "It's about accepting that people think differently — and that's normal."

**Questions:**
1. Why did many neurodivergent people struggle at work?
2. How are some hiring programs different?
3. What strengths are mentioned?
4. What do advocates say the goal should be?

## 📝 Writing

Write a reflective paragraph (150–180 words): "A person who changed the way I see the world". Use superlatives with present perfect, has taught me and the first time.

**Model:**

> The person who has changed the way I see the world the most is my grandfather, Seu João. He grew up on a farm in Bahia and left school when he was ten, but he's the wisest person I've ever met. When I was a teenager, I thought success meant money and a big title. My grandfather taught me a different lesson. Every morning, he would share his coffee with the street sweepers and ask about their families. "Everyone has something to teach you," he used to say. The turning point for me was at his 80th birthday party. It was the first time I'd seen so many different people in one room — doctors, farmers, children, neighbors — all there because he had been kind to them. That day, I realized that the richest people aren't the ones with the most money, but the ones with the most connections. He has taught me to respect everyone, and it's the most valuable lesson I've ever learned.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Linguagem inclusiva em inglês

Em ambientes profissionais de língua inglesa, há atenção crescente à linguagem inclusiva: **they/them** como pronome singular neutro, **chairperson** em vez de chairman, **people with disabilities** em vez de "the disabled". Não é sobre regras rígidas, mas sobre respeito: quando não souber, **pergunte como a pessoa prefere**.
