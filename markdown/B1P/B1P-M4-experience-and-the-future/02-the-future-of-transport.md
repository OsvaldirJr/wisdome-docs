## 🧩 Grammar: Previsões com graus de certeza

No B1+, em vez de usar só **will**, mostre **o quanto você tem certeza**:

**100% →** will definitely / be bound to (com certeza vai) / certainly will
"Electric cars **will definitely** become cheaper." / "There**'s bound to** be traffic."

**~80% →** will probably / be likely to + verbo
"Most new cars **will probably** be electric by 2035." / "Prices **are likely to** fall."

**~50% →** may / might / could + verbo (talvez)
"Flying taxis **might** be available in some cities." / "It **could** take decades."

**~20% →** probably won't / be unlikely to / I doubt (that)...
"People **probably won't** stop driving completely." / "It's **unlikely to** happen soon."

**0% →** definitely won't / there's no way...
"Cars **definitely won't** disappear by 2030."

**Posição dos advérbios:**
- afirmativa: **will + advérbio** → "It **will probably** rain."
- negativa: **advérbio + won't** → "It **probably won't** rain." (❌ "It won't probably")

**Futuro contínuo e perfeito (reconhecimento):**
- "In 2040, we**'ll be traveling** in autonomous cars." (ação em andamento num momento futuro)
- "By 2035, many countries **will have banned** new gasoline cars." (concluído até um ponto futuro)

| Certeza | Estrutura | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| 100% | will definitely / be bound to | Cities will definitely change. |
| ~80% | will probably / be likely to | EVs are likely to get cheaper. |
| ~50% | may / might / could | Flying taxis might appear. |
| ~20% | probably won't / be unlikely to | It's unlikely to happen soon. |
| 0% | definitely won't | Cars definitely won't disappear. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L2/example-01.m4a) Electric buses are likely to replace diesel buses in most big cities. — _Ônibus elétricos provavelmente vão substituir os ônibus a diesel na maioria das cidades grandes._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L2/example-02.m4a) Self-driving cars might be safer, but people probably won't trust them for a while. — _Carros autônomos podem ser mais seguros, mas as pessoas provavelmente não vão confiar neles por um tempo._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L2/example-03.m4a) By 2040, many cities will have closed their centers to private cars. — _Até 2040, muitas cidades terão fechado seus centros para carros particulares._

> 💡 **Dica:** **Be likely to** é muito usado em notícias e relatórios: "The new law **is likely to** affect millions of drivers." É uma ótima forma de soar mais sofisticado do que "will probably".

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ It won't probably happen.  
  ✅ **It probably won't happen.**  
  Na negativa, o advérbio vem antes de won't.
- ❌ Prices are likely to falling.  
  ✅ **Prices are likely to fall.**  
  Be likely to + verbo base.
- ❌ Maybe flying cars will might exist.  
  ✅ **Flying cars might exist. / Maybe flying cars will exist.**  
  Não combine dois modais (will + might).

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Probably e definitely

Na fala rápida, **probably** vira "**PRÓB-li**" (quase duas sílabas) e **definitely** vira "**DÉF-net-li**". **Likely** = "LÁIK-li". A força está sempre no começo dessas palavras.

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L2/pron-01.m4a) It'll PROBably rain.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L2/pron-02.m4a) They'll DEFinitely win.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L2/pron-03.m4a) It's LIKEly to change.

## 📖 Reading — The 15-minute city [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L2/reading.m4a)

What if everything you needed — work, school, shops, a doctor, a park — was within a 15-minute walk or bike ride from your home? That's the idea behind the "15-minute city", a concept developed by the urbanist Carlos Moreno and adopted by Paris and other cities around the world. Supporters argue that it's likely to reduce traffic, pollution and stress, while making neighborhoods more lively and social. In Paris, the city has created hundreds of kilometers of new bike lanes and turned some streets around schools into car-free zones. Critics, however, say the idea may not work in places with low population density, where shops and jobs are far apart. Others worry that it could increase housing prices in well-designed neighborhoods, pushing poorer residents out. Some online conspiracy theories have even claimed, without evidence, that 15-minute cities are a plan to restrict people's freedom of movement. The debate is bound to continue — but as cities grow and the climate crisis worsens, many planners believe that shorter, greener journeys are the future.

**Questions:**
1. What is a 15-minute city?
2. What has Paris done?
3. Where might the idea not work?
4. What social risk do critics mention?

## 📝 Writing

Write a short report (150–180 words): "Transport in my city in 2045". Make predictions with at least 6 different expressions of certainty.

**Model:**

> Transport in Curitiba in 2045  
>   
> Curitiba has always been a pioneer in public transport, so its future is likely to be exciting. First, the city's famous bus rapid transit system will definitely continue, but the buses are bound to be fully electric and much quieter. Second, the number of bike lanes is likely to double, because more young people prefer cycling to driving. Self-driving cars might become common on highways around the city, but I doubt they will be popular in the busy center, where pedestrians and cyclists make traffic unpredictable. Flying taxis could appear for business travelers, but they're unlikely to be affordable for most people. On the other hand, private cars probably won't disappear completely, especially for families in the suburbs. In my opinion, the biggest change will be cultural: by 2045, many residents will have stopped seeing the car as a symbol of status, and the city will probably be greener, quieter and more pleasant to live in.
