## 🧩 Grammar: Linguagem de telefone e mensagens

Ao telefone, a linguagem é bem **fixa** — aprenda em **blocos**:

**1. Identificar-se:** em inglês, diga **"This is..."** (não "I am..."): "Hello, **this is** Pedro from Alfa Bank." / Para perguntar quem fala: "**Who's calling, please?**" / "**May I ask who's calling?**"

**2. Pedir para falar com alguém:** "**Could I speak to**...?" / "**I'm calling about**..." / "**I'm returning** Ms. Lee's **call**."

**3. Pedidos educados:** **will / would / could** — "**Could you** ask her to call me back?" / "**Would you** mind spelling that?"

**4. Ofertas com will (decisão na hora):** "**I'll** put you through." / "**I'll** tell him you called." / "**I'll** call back later."

**5. Passar recados (reported requests — introdução):**
- Ela disse: "Call me back." → **She asked you to call her back.**
- Ele disse: "I'll be late." → **He said (that) he'd be late.**
(Você vai aprofundar o reported speech no Módulo 6!)

**6. Estrutura de uma boa mensagem de voz:**
1. Cumprimento + **quem é** (nome + empresa)
2. **Hora/data** da ligação
3. **Motivo** da ligação
4. **Ação** pedida ("Could you call me back...?")
5. **Número** — falado **devagar**, de preferência **duas vezes**
6. Despedida

| Situação | Frase |
|---|---|
| Atender (empresa) | Good morning, Alfa Bank, Laura speaking. How can I help? |
| Identificar-se | Hi, this is Pedro from TechSol. |
| Pedir alguém | Could I speak to Mr. Davis, please? |
| Transferir | Hold on, please. I'll put you through. |
| Pessoa indisponível | I'm afraid she's in a meeting. Can I take a message? |
| Problema técnico | Sorry, you're breaking up. Could you repeat that? |
| Encerrar | Thanks for calling. Have a good day. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L4/example-01.m4a) Hi, this is Mariana Lopes from Green Energy. I'm calling about the proposal I sent on Monday. — _Oi, aqui é a Mariana Lopes, da Green Energy. Estou ligando sobre a proposta que enviei na segunda._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L4/example-02.m4a) I'm afraid Mr. Davis is out of the office today. Would you like to leave a message? — _Infelizmente o Sr. Davis está fora do escritório hoje. Gostaria de deixar um recado?_
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L4/example-03.m4a) Sorry, the line is really bad. I'll call you back in a minute. — _Desculpe, a linha está muito ruim. Te ligo de volta em um minuto._

> 💡 **Dica:** Para soletrar ao telefone, use o **alfabeto fonético** (NATO): A de Alpha, B de Bravo, C de Charlie, D de Delta, E de Echo... "S as in Sierra, O as in Oscar, U as in Uniform..."

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ Hello, I am Pedro. (ao telefone)  
  ✅ **Hello, this is Pedro.**  
  Ao telefone, identifique-se com "This is...".
- ❌ Can you call me back to the number...?  
  ✅ **Can you call me back on/at 555-2190?**  
  Número de telefone: on (UK) ou at (US).
- ❌ She said me to call her.  
  ✅ **She asked me to call her. / She told me to call her.**  
  Say não tem objeto direto de pessoa; use ask/tell + objeto + to.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Números de telefone

Fale números em **blocos de 3 ou 4**, com pausa entre eles e entonação subindo no fim de cada bloco (menos o último): "five-five-five ↗ / two-one-nine-oh ↘". **0** = "oh" ou "zero"; **44** = "double four".

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L4/pron-01.m4a) My number is five-five-five ↗, two-one-nine-oh ↘.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L4/pron-02.m4a) That's double-four, seven-oh-two.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L4/pron-03.m4a) S as in Sierra, O as in Oscar.

## 📖 Reading — Why we hate phone calls [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M4-L4/reading.m4a)

For many young professionals, an unexpected phone call feels almost like an emergency. Surveys in the UK and the US have found that a large number of people under 35 avoid answering calls from unknown numbers, and many feel anxious when they have to make a call. Some even rehearse what they're going to say before dialing. Why? Researchers point to several reasons. Text messages and emails give us time to think and edit; phone calls happen in real time, with no chance to delete a mistake. Calls can also feel intrusive: they interrupt whatever you're doing. And for people speaking a second language, the lack of facial expressions and gestures makes understanding much harder. However, many managers say that phone calls are still the fastest way to solve complex problems and build relationships. A five-minute call can save twenty emails. The solution? Practice. Experts suggest preparing a few key phrases, keeping a notepad ready and remembering that it's completely normal to say: "Sorry, could you repeat that?"

**Questions:**
1. What do many people under 35 avoid?
2. Why do text messages feel easier?
3. Why are calls harder in a second language?
4. Why do managers still value phone calls?

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — Ligar sem avisar?

Em muitos ambientes profissionais de língua inglesa, ligar sem avisar é cada vez menos comum. É educado mandar uma mensagem antes: **"Do you have five minutes for a quick call?"** ou **"Can I give you a ring?"** (UK). E no WhatsApp, áudios longos de trabalho podem ser malvistos — prefira texto ou uma chamada marcada.
