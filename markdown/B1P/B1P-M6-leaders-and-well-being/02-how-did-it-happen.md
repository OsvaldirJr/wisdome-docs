## 🧩 Grammar: Past perfect simple x continuous

Os dois olham para **antes de um ponto do passado**:

**Past perfect simple (had + particípio)** → ação **concluída** antes de outro momento passado; foco no **resultado** ou na **quantidade**:
- "By the time she was 25, she **had written** three books."
- "When I arrived, the meeting **had** already **started**."
- "He **had never seen** the sea before that trip."

**Past perfect continuous (had been + -ing)** → atividade **em andamento por um período** até um momento do passado; foco na **duração** ou na **causa** de algo:
- "She **had been working** as a nurse **for ten years** when she decided to study medicine."
- "His eyes were red because he **had been crying**."
- "They **had been trying** to have a baby for years before their daughter was born."

Mesma lógica do present perfect simple x continuous — só que **um passo para trás**.

**Conectores úteis:**
- **by the time** + past simple, ... past perfect: "By the time we arrived, they **had left**."
- **before / after / when / once / until**
- **already, just, never... before, for, since**

**Estrutura de uma biografia:** past simple para a linha principal dos fatos; past perfect para voltar no tempo e explicar o contexto; past continuous para cenas: "In 2008, she **launched** her app. She **had been developing** it for three years, and she **had been rejected** by fifteen investors. On the day of the launch, she **was** still **sleeping** on her friend's sofa."

| Past perfect simple (resultado) | Past perfect continuous (duração/causa) |
|---|---|
| She had written 5 novels by 2010. | She had been writing for 20 years. |
| He had saved R$50,000. | He had been saving for years. |
| They had finished the project. | They had been working all night (so they were tired). |
| I had never been to Asia. | I had been planning the trip for months. |

**Examples:**
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M6-L2/example-01.m4a) By the time Pelé was 17, he had already won a World Cup. — _Quando Pelé tinha 17 anos, já tinha ganhado uma Copa do Mundo._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M6-L2/example-02.m4a) She had been teaching for twenty years when she wrote her first novel. — _Ela dava aula havia vinte anos quando escreveu seu primeiro romance._
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M6-L2/example-03.m4a) The ground was wet because it had been raining all night. — _O chão estava molhado porque tinha chovido a noite toda._

> 💡 **Dica:** Não exagere no past perfect: use-o só quando precisa deixar claro que algo aconteceu **antes**. Se a ordem já está clara com **before/after**, o past simple basta: "After she **graduated**, she **moved** to Lisbon."

## ⚠️ Atenção, brasileiro!

- ❌ When I got to the airport, the plane already left.  
  ✅ **When I got to the airport, the plane had already left.**  
  O avião saiu antes da chegada: past perfect.
- ❌ She had been knowing him for years.  
  ✅ **She had known him for years.**  
  Know é stative: só simple.
- ❌ He had been writing five books by 2010.  
  ✅ **He had written five books by 2010.**  
  Quantidade (five books) = past perfect simple.

## 🗣️ Pronunciation: Had been = "ádbin"

**Had** é muito fraco na fala: "She'd been working" = "shíd-bin-UÓR-kin". **Had** sozinho às vezes vira só "d": "By the time I arrived, they'd left" = "dêid léft". Preste atenção ao particípio.

- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M6-L2/pron-01.m4a) She'd been working there for years.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M6-L2/pron-02.m4a) By the time we arrived, they'd left.
- [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M6-L2/pron-03.m4a) He'd never seen the sea before.

## 📖 Reading — Late bloomers [🔊](../../../audio/B1P/B1P-M6-L2/reading.m4a)

We often celebrate young geniuses: the teenage tech founder, the 20-year-old Olympic champion. But many remarkable people achieved success much later. Before she published the first Harry Potter book at 32, J.K. Rowling had been rejected by numerous publishers and had been living as a single mother on very little money. The fashion designer Vera Wang had worked as a journalist and figure skater before she designed her first dress at 40. Colonel Sanders had tried many careers — and failed at several — before he started franchising his fried chicken in his sixties. In Brazil, the writer Cora Coralina had been writing poetry for most of her life, but her first book wasn't published until she was 75. Researchers who study creativity suggest that there are different types of creators: some make their breakthroughs early through bold experiments, while others need decades of accumulated experience. The lesson? If you haven't found your path by 30 — or 50 — you're in very good company.

**Questions:**
1. What had happened to J.K. Rowling before she published Harry Potter?
2. What had Vera Wang done before designing dresses?
3. How old was Cora Coralina when her first book was published?
4. What do researchers say about different types of creators?

## 📝 Writing

Write a short biography (150–180 words) of a person you admire. Use past simple, past continuous, past perfect simple and continuous, and at least 3 time connectors.

**Model:**

> Cora Coralina: a poet who never gave up  
>   
> Ana Lins dos Guimarães Peixoto Bretas, known as Cora Coralina, was born in 1889 in the city of Goiás. She had only four years of formal education, but she had been writing poems and short stories since she was a teenager.  
>   
> For most of her life, however, few people read her work. She got married, moved to the state of São Paulo and raised her children. To support her family, she sold books and, later, she made and sold sweets. All this time, she had been writing in secret.  
>   
> The turning point came in 1965. By then, she had returned to her hometown, where she was living in an old house by the river. That year, her first book was finally published — she was 75 years old. Years later, the famous poet Carlos Drummond de Andrade praised her work, and she became known all over Brazil.  
>   
> By the time she died in 1985, Cora Coralina had become a symbol of resilience. Her story reminds us that it's never too late to share your voice.

## 🌎 Cultura & Curiosidade — "It's never too late"

Expressões em inglês sobre recomeçar: **"It's never too late"**, **"Better late than never"**, **"Age is just a number"** e **"a late bloomer"** (alguém que floresce tarde). Em currículos internacionais, mudanças de carreira são cada vez mais valorizadas como **diverse experience**.

## ✅ Resumo
- had + particípio = concluído antes de outro ponto do passado.
- had been + -ing = duração/causa antes de um ponto do passado.
- By the time + past simple, past perfect.
- Biografia: past simple (linha principal) + past perfect (contexto).
